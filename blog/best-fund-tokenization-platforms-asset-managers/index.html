Best Fund Tokenization Platforms for Asset Managers in 2026

Compare fund tokenization platforms by regulated roles, investor onboarding, transfer controls, administration, custody, distribution and redemptions.

Reviewed and updated by FluidRWA · September 14, 2026

Short answer

Asset managers should shortlist fund tokenization platforms by operating model rather than headline features. Securitize is relevant for regulated US digital-securities and fund workflows; Tokeny for permissioned ERC-3643 infrastructure; Zoniqx for modular issuance and lifecycle tooling; Taurus for bank-oriented custody and tokenization infrastructure; and Libre for tokenized fund issuance and distribution. The right platform must fit the fund domicile, administrator, transfer controls, custody, subscriptions, redemptions and target investors.

Start with the Fund Operating Model

Before requesting demonstrations, document:

  • fund domicile, legal form and offering exemptions
  • investor types and target jurisdictions
  • authoritative ownership record and transfer-agent responsibilities
  • administrator, NAV and accounting workflow
  • subscription, capital-call, distribution and redemption process
  • custody model for fund interests, cash and digital assets
  • wallet model and investor recovery process
  • primary distribution and permitted secondary transfers

A platform can automate an agreed operating model. It cannot resolve an unclear legal structure or assign regulated responsibilities on the asset manager's behalf.

Where Each Platform May Fit

Securitize

Securitize combines tokenization technology with identified regulated functions across its group. It is a natural first investigation for established managers seeking a US-oriented digital-securities workflow. Buyers should separate each entity and agreement, then confirm asset eligibility, transfer-agent scope, custody connectivity and distribution.

Tokeny

Tokeny centers its infrastructure on ERC-3643 and identity-linked permissioned transfers. It may fit banks, administrators and asset managers building modular EVM-based fund infrastructure. Confirm who operates identity registries, compliance rules, recovery, servicing and investor support.

Zoniqx

Zoniqx presents modular tools spanning issuance, identity, lifecycle and distribution. It may fit managers seeking multi-chain flexibility, but buyers should verify production references, licensed-party dependencies and which modules are generally available. Its ERC-7518-related positioning should be read with the proposal's current Review status in mind.

Taurus

Taurus is relevant to banks and regulated financial institutions evaluating custody, tokenization and digital asset operations in one infrastructure relationship. Confirm which modules, entities, networks and service responsibilities apply to the proposed fund.

Libre

Libre is relevant to asset managers considering tokenized fund issuance and distribution to eligible investors. Confirm fund eligibility, administrator and custodian relationships, transfer restrictions, investor access and redemption mechanics.

Proof-of-Concept Tests

  1. Onboard one eligible and one ineligible investor.
  2. Process a subscription through final reconciled ownership records.
  3. Apply a lockup and reject an unauthorized transfer.
  4. Update NAV and generate investor reporting.
  5. Process a distribution with withholding and failed-payment handling.
  6. Complete a redemption and reconcile token, cash and administrator records.
  7. Recover an investor wallet without weakening identity controls.
  8. Export the complete register, documents, rules and audit history.

Final Recommendation

Do not issue an RFP titled simply "tokenize our fund." Define the fund, investors, regulated roles, records, cash flows and lifecycle events first. Then compare RWA tokenization platforms, institutional custody providers and fund administration and transfer agent providers as one operating stack.

FAQ

What is a fund tokenization platform?

A fund tokenization platform helps represent fund interests as digital tokens and connect issuance with investor onboarding, transfer controls, subscriptions, redemptions, reporting and lifecycle administration.

Which fund tokenization platform is best?

There is no universal best platform. Fit depends on fund domicile, investor type, regulated roles, transfer agent or administrator, custody, token standard, distribution and redemption process.

Does tokenization replace a fund administrator?

Usually not. Tokenization software may automate parts of the workflow, but NAV, books and records, investor servicing, reporting and regulatory responsibilities still need clearly assigned owners.

Can tokenized funds use stablecoins for subscriptions?

Some structures can support stablecoin settlement, but the issuer must address eligibility, custody, cash controls, reconciliation, redemption and applicable legal requirements.

What should an asset manager test?

Test onboarding, rejected subscriptions, transfer restrictions, NAV updates, distributions, redemptions, wallet recovery, reconciliation, reporting and data export.

Are tokenized fund interests automatically liquid?

No. Liquidity depends on eligible buyers, transfer rules, market access, pricing, disclosures and available trading or redemption mechanisms.
