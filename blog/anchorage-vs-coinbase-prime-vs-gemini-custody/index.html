Anchorage vs Coinbase Prime vs Gemini Custody: Institutional Custody Compared

Compare Anchorage Digital, Coinbase Prime and Gemini Custody for institutional crypto custody, trading access, compliance, insurance and asset support.

Reviewed and updated by FluidRWA · August 12, 2026

Short answer

Anchorage Digital is often strongest for federally chartered institutional custody and bank-grade controls. Coinbase Prime is strongest when custody, execution, financing, staking and onchain access need to sit in one prime brokerage environment. Gemini Custody is a practical regulated cold-storage option for institutions that want custody with exchange connectivity.

Institutional custody is not just about where private keys sit. It affects how assets move, who can approve transactions, how audit evidence is produced, how trading works, whether staking is available, how fiat settlement is handled and whether the institution can explain the risk model to investors, regulators and internal committees.

Anchorage Digital, Coinbase Prime and Gemini Custody are all recognizable names in institutional crypto custody. But they fit different buyer situations.

Short Answer

Choose Anchorage Digital if qualified custody, regulated institutional structure and bank-grade controls are central to the mandate.

Choose Coinbase Prime if custody is only one part of the workflow and the institution also needs execution, financing, futures, staking and integrated portfolio operations.

Choose Gemini Custody if the buyer wants regulated cold storage, segregated addresses, exchange connectivity and a more straightforward custody-led setup.

What Anchorage Digital Is Good For

Anchorage Digital is built for institutions that need custody to withstand a legal, compliance and operational review. It positions Anchorage Digital Bank N.A. as the first federally chartered crypto bank in the United States and emphasizes qualified custody, institutional security, fiat and crypto custody, and global custody through Anchorage Digital Singapore.

Anchorage is especially relevant for:

  • Asset managers
  • Banks
  • RIAs and wealth platforms
  • Tokenized fund issuers
  • Stablecoin and settlement programs
  • Institutions that need custody, staking, trading and governance under a regulated operating model

The strongest Anchorage use case is not simply "hold crypto." It is "hold crypto in a structure that an institutional committee can understand." That means governance, transaction authorization, auditability, regulatory status and control evidence are part of the product.

Where Anchorage May Not Be Ideal

Anchorage may be more than a small crypto-native team needs if the buyer only wants basic self-serve custody or low-cost exchange storage. It is best evaluated when regulatory posture, operational resilience and institutional support matter enough to justify an enterprise procurement process.

Buyers should confirm:

  • Supported assets and chains for their jurisdiction
  • Whether the specific legal entity meets their qualified custodian requirement
  • Insurance coverage and exclusions
  • Transaction approval flow
  • Fiat custody availability
  • Reporting and API access
  • Staking and governance support by asset

What Coinbase Prime Is Good For

Coinbase Prime is not just a custody product. It is a broader institutional operating system for custody, execution, financing, futures, staking and onchain activity. That makes it especially useful for funds, institutions and sophisticated trading desks that do not want custody and execution to live in completely separate systems.

Coinbase Prime is especially relevant for:

  • Funds that trade actively
  • Market participants needing custody plus execution
  • Institutions that want staking and financing in one workflow
  • Teams that value liquidity access and integrated reporting
  • Organizations already connected to Coinbase institutional infrastructure

The advantage is operational compression. A team can hold assets, trade, finance positions, stake supported assets and manage institutional workflows through one major platform.

Where Coinbase Prime May Not Be Ideal

The integrated nature of Coinbase Prime can be a strength or a concern. Some organizations intentionally separate custody, trading and financing vendors to reduce concentration risk. Others may have jurisdiction, asset, counterparty or internal policy constraints.

Buyers should ask:

  • Which custody entity applies to their geography?
  • Which assets are custody-only versus tradeable?
  • How custody, exchange and financing risks are separated
  • Whether portfolio margin or financing is required
  • What reporting is available for audits
  • Whether the organization wants one prime platform or a multi-vendor model

What Gemini Custody Is Good For

Gemini Custody is a regulated custody option with strong emphasis on cold storage, segregation, insurance, compliance and ease of use. Gemini says customer assets are held using unique digital addresses that can be independently verified and that custody integrates with the Gemini exchange dashboard.

Gemini Custody is especially relevant for:

  • Institutions that prioritize cold storage
  • Smaller institutions that want regulated custody without a heavy prime brokerage setup
  • Funds or companies that want custody plus exchange connectivity
  • Teams that need monthly statements and API access
  • Organizations comfortable with Gemini's asset and jurisdiction coverage

The clearest Gemini fit is where custody is the main need and trading integration is helpful, but the buyer does not need a fully integrated prime brokerage stack.

Where Gemini May Not Be Ideal

Gemini Custody may not be the best fit for buyers that need broad prime financing, complex institutional trading, specialized tokenization workflows or custody across unusual assets. Buyers should also review current fees, minimums, insurance details and jurisdiction-specific availability.

How to Choose for Tokenization

Tokenized asset projects should not choose custody based only on brand recognition. The relevant custody questions are more specific:

  • Will the custodian support the token standard and chain used by the issuer?
  • Can custody addresses be tied to investor, fund or SPV records?
  • Can the custodian support transfer restrictions or approved wallet flows?
  • Are reports usable for NAV, audit, reserve verification or investor statements?
  • Does the custodian support stablecoin settlement?
  • Can the custodian integrate with the tokenization platform?
  • Is staking relevant or prohibited?
  • Who approves transfers, and how is approval evidenced?

Best Fit by Scenario

Use Anchorage Digital when the committee cares most about regulatory posture, qualified custody, institutional control and bank-grade infrastructure.

Use Coinbase Prime when the team needs custody connected to trading, financing, staking and broader institutional execution.

Use Gemini Custody when the team wants regulated cold storage, segregated custody addresses and a simpler custody-led path.

FAQ

Which is better: Anchorage Digital, Coinbase Prime or Gemini Custody?

It depends on the operating model. Anchorage is strong for bank-grade qualified custody, Coinbase Prime is strong for custody plus execution and financing, and Gemini Custody is strong for regulated cold storage with exchange connectivity.

Which custodian is best for tokenized assets?

The best custodian depends on supported assets, legal structure, jurisdiction, transfer controls, settlement model and integration requirements. Tokenized securities, funds and stablecoin workflows may need different custody capabilities.

Does Coinbase Prime include custody?

Yes. Coinbase Prime is an institutional platform that includes custody as part of a broader prime brokerage environment covering execution, financing, staking and other institutional workflows.

Is Anchorage Digital a qualified custodian?

Anchorage Digital Bank N.A. describes itself as a federally chartered crypto bank and qualified custodian for institutional digital assets. Buyers should verify current entity, jurisdiction and asset availability.

Is Gemini Custody insured?

Gemini states that Gemini Custody maintains certain digital asset insurance coverage, including cold-storage insurance, but buyers should review current fee schedules, coverage details and exclusions directly.

What should institutions compare before choosing a custodian?

Compare legal entity, regulatory status, asset support, insurance, governance controls, API access, transaction approval flow, audit reports, settlement options, staking access and reporting.

Should custody and trading be with the same vendor?

Sometimes. A combined custody and execution platform can reduce operational friction, but segregating custody and execution may be preferred for risk control, governance or regulatory reasons.
