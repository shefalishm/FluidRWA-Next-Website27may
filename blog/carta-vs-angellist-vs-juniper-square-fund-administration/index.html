Carta vs AngelList vs Juniper Square: Fund Administration Comparison

Compare Carta, AngelList and Juniper Square for fund administration, investor portals, LP onboarding, tax, KYC, reporting and private market operations.

Reviewed and updated by FluidRWA · August 9, 2026

Short answer

Carta is strongest for funds that want fund management software and administration connected to equity, portfolio and cap table workflows. AngelList is strongest for venture funds, rolling funds and SPVs that want formation, admin, investor onboarding, compliance and tax support in one platform. Juniper Square is strongest for private markets GPs, real assets, private equity, private credit and larger firms needing connected fund administration and investor management.

Tokenization Does Not Remove Fund Operations

Tokenized funds still need investor onboarding, subscriptions, capital calls, distributions, tax records, investor statements, reporting, compliance checks and ownership records. A token can make some workflows more programmable, but it does not replace fund administration.

Carta, AngelList and Juniper Square are all relevant to private market fund operations, but they serve different buyer profiles. A venture SPV, a private credit fund, a real estate GP and a tokenized feeder fund should not evaluate them with the same checklist.

Quick Comparison

| Provider | Strongest fit | Less ideal when | Buyer should verify | |---|---|---|---| | Carta | Funds that want fund management software, administration and equity-connected workflows | The fund needs a highly customized global private markets administrator with complex cross-border structures | Fund admin scope, tax, K-1s, portfolio valuation, cap table links, investor portal, pricing | | AngelList | Venture funds, rolling funds, syndicates and SPVs needing formation, admin, investor onboarding, compliance and tax support | The strategy is not venture-oriented or requires complex institutional private markets operations | Structure eligibility, pricing, platform-advised vs self-advised options, KYC, USDC availability | | Juniper Square | Private markets GPs, real assets, PE, private credit and larger managers needing connected administration and investor services | The buyer wants a small self-serve venture fund launch at minimal setup complexity | Supported fund structures, jurisdiction coverage, fund accounting, investor portal, AML/KYC, treasury |

Short Answer

Choose Carta if your fund operations connect closely to equity, portfolio, valuations, fund accounting and management company workflows.

Choose AngelList if you are a venture fund, rolling fund or SPV manager that wants an integrated fund formation and administration platform.

Choose Juniper Square if you are a private markets GP with larger or more complex fund administration and investor management needs.

Carta: Best for Fund Management Software and Equity-Connected Workflows

Carta’s fund management offering emphasizes an end-to-end suite for fund management, including fund administration, fund tax, fund formations, forecasting, portfolio valuations and management company tools.

Carta may fit:

  • venture funds and private funds already using Carta workflows
  • managers that want software plus administration support
  • funds needing portfolio valuation and fund reporting
  • firms that want equity, ownership and fund data connected
  • emerging or scaling managers that want a recognizable platform

Carta’s key advantage is operational integration. If a manager already uses Carta for cap table, valuations or equity-related workflows, adding fund operations may reduce fragmentation.

The diligence issue is depth. Buyers should confirm how much is software, how much is service, what tax support is included, how custom fund structures are handled and whether tokenized investor records are supported.

Questions to ask Carta:

  • Which fund types and jurisdictions are supported?
  • What fund administration services are included versus extra?
  • How are capital calls, distributions and investor statements handled?
  • Does the investor portal support the LP experience we need?
  • Can wallet information or tokenized investor references be tracked?
  • How are valuations, tax and management company workflows priced?

AngelList: Best for Venture Funds, Rolling Funds and SPVs

AngelList is especially relevant for venture-oriented fund structures. Its public materials describe fund administration, fund formation, regulatory filings, tax, investor closing, KYC/AML checks, LP portals, USDC funding rails through a third party and support for venture funds, rolling funds and syndicates.

AngelList may fit:

  • emerging venture managers
  • rolling funds
  • syndicates and SPVs
  • managers that want formation, admin and investor onboarding bundled
  • GP teams that want standardized venture fund infrastructure
  • managers that value access to AngelList’s broader venture ecosystem

AngelList is often strongest where the fund model fits its platform. That can create speed and simplicity. It may be less ideal for private credit, real estate, infrastructure or complex global fund structures that do not fit a venture platform model.

Questions to ask AngelList:

  • Which structure is appropriate: venture fund, rolling fund or syndicate?
  • What is included in full-service fund administration?
  • Which filings, tax forms and KYC/AML processes are handled?
  • Can investors fund using USDC, and in which cases?
  • What legal documents are standardized versus custom?
  • What does the 10-year pricing model include?

Juniper Square: Best for Private Markets GP Scale

Juniper Square is positioned for private markets GPs across real assets, private equity, private credit and venture capital. Its public materials emphasize connected fund administration, fund accounting, investor services, AML/KYC, treasury services, waterfall modeling and an LP portal used across large volumes of LP accounts and private market capital.

Juniper Square may fit:

  • real estate and real asset managers
  • private equity and private credit GPs
  • larger venture firms
  • managers with complex entities and LP communications
  • firms that need outsourced fund accounting and investor services
  • teams that want a modern private markets portal and administration partner

Juniper Square’s strength is scale and private markets specialization. For a tokenized private markets product, this matters because the administrator must understand investor servicing, reporting, distributions and fund structures beyond simple startup SPVs.

Questions to ask Juniper Square:

  • Which asset classes and fund structures are supported?
  • How are fund accounting and investor services divided?
  • Does the platform support AML/KYC and treasury workflows?
  • Can it support cross-border or Luxembourg-domiciled structures?
  • How does the LP portal handle reporting and document access?
  • Can tokenization-related data be stored or integrated?

What Tokenized Funds Should Add to the Standard Checklist

Traditional fund administration diligence is necessary but not sufficient for tokenized funds. Add these questions:

  • Can the administrator support wallet-linked investor records?
  • Can token ownership and legal ownership be reconciled?
  • How are secondary transfers handled?
  • How are transfer restrictions recorded?
  • Can stablecoin payments be reconciled with fiat bank records?
  • Can subscriptions and redemptions be matched to KYC status?
  • Can investor statements show both legal rights and token references?
  • What happens if the tokenization platform changes?

Which Provider Should You Shortlist?

Choose Carta if equity-connected fund software and administration are central to the operating model.

Choose AngelList if the fund is venture-oriented and you want integrated formation, admin, tax and investor onboarding.

Choose Juniper Square if the fund is larger, private-market-oriented, real-asset-heavy or operationally complex.

For tokenized funds, the fund administrator should be evaluated alongside legal counsel, transfer agent, custody provider, KYC vendor, tokenization platform and payments provider.

FAQ

Which is better: Carta, AngelList or Juniper Square?

Carta is often strongest for fund management software and equity-connected operations, AngelList for venture funds and SPVs with integrated formation and admin, and Juniper Square for private markets GPs needing fund administration, investor services and LP portal scale.

Do tokenized funds still need fund administration?

Yes. Tokenization does not remove accounting, investor records, capital calls, distributions, tax, statements, compliance checks or investor communications.

Is fund administration the same as transfer agency?

No. Fund administration focuses on accounting, reporting and fund operations. Transfer agency focuses on maintaining investor ownership records, subscriptions, redemptions and transfer processing. Some providers or partners may support both.

What should a tokenized fund ask a fund administrator?

Ask whether they support digital subscriptions, KYC/AML, wallet-linked investors, stablecoin payments, tokenized ownership records, transfer restrictions, tax reporting and custody integrations.

Does AngelList support USDC funding?

AngelList public support materials reference USDC funding payment rails through a third-party service provider for certain investor closing workflows. Buyers should verify current availability.

Does Juniper Square support real assets?

Juniper Square publicly positions its platform for private markets including real assets, private equity, private credit and venture capital.

Does Carta provide fund administration?

Carta offers fund management software and services including fund administration, tax, formations, portfolio valuations and management company workflows.
