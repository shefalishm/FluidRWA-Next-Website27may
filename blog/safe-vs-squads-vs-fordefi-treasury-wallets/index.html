Safe vs Squads vs Fordefi: Treasury Wallet Comparison

Compare Safe, Squads and Fordefi for multisig treasury management, MPC wallets, transaction policy, Solana, EVM, DeFi and institutional operations.

Reviewed and updated by FluidRWA · August 21, 2026

Short answer

Safe is a strong fit for EVM smart-account multisig and onchain treasury governance. Squads is purpose-built for Solana and SVM multisig operations. Fordefi suits institutions needing MPC, granular policy, DeFi and CeFi connectivity across chains.

Short Answer

Safe, Squads and Fordefi protect treasury operations with different control models and network emphases.

  • Safe is the natural choice for EVM smart-account multisig and transparent onchain governance.
  • Squads is purpose-built around Solana and SVM multisig treasury operations.
  • Fordefi targets institutions needing MPC wallets, granular policies and connectivity across DeFi, centralized venues and many networks.

The decision is not simply multisig versus MPC. It is a choice about chain support, policy administration, transaction clarity, integrations, recovery and who carries operational responsibility.

Safe

Safe uses smart accounts to enforce multi-owner approval and programmable controls. Its transparency and composability make it a common treasury foundation in EVM ecosystems.

Good for: DAOs, protocol treasuries, EVM asset control, transparent approvals and integrations with onchain applications.

Watch-outs: multisig is not automatically secure. Owner devices, signer diversity, modules, guards, frontends and recovery procedures all need review.

Squads

Squads focuses on multisig and treasury operations in the Solana ecosystem. That specialization can produce a more natural fit for Solana programs, tokens and teams than an EVM-first product.

Good for: Solana/SVM treasuries, program upgrades, team approvals and operational governance.

Watch-outs: confirm every required network, integration and transaction type. Cross-chain organizations may need an additional control plane.

Fordefi

Fordefi provides an institutional MPC wallet platform, policy engine, DeFi gateway and wallet-as-a-service capabilities. It targets organizations that need transaction clarity and controlled access across decentralized and centralized venues.

Good for: trading firms, funds, market makers, multi-chain treasury teams and embedded institutional wallet products.

Watch-outs: map policy ownership, quorum governance, device security, API access and vendor dependency. Institutional depth also means a more involved implementation.

Buyer Scenarios

EVM protocol treasury

Safe is the logical starting point. Use independent signer devices, separate policy administration and test all enabled modules.

Solana-native organization

Squads is the natural lead candidate. Test program interactions, signer replacement and emergency governance before funding the production account.

Multi-chain fund or trading desk

Fordefi is the strongest initial fit where policies, exchange connectivity, APIs and DeFi transaction controls need a unified institutional workflow.

Governance and Recovery Checklist

  • How many signers are required for each transaction class?
  • Can the same person control multiple signer devices?
  • Who can change policy or add an owner?
  • Are high-value and contract-interaction policies different?
  • How are transaction simulations and decoded actions presented?
  • What happens when a signer leaves the company?
  • Can the treasury operate during a vendor or frontend outage?
  • How are backups and recovery shares protected?
  • Are modules, integrations and APIs independently reviewed?
  • Can complete approval and transaction evidence be exported?

Verdict

Choose Safe for EVM smart-account governance, Squads for Solana-native multisig operations and Fordefi for institutional MPC, policy and cross-venue connectivity. The best platform is the one whose recovery and administrative controls your team can operate correctly under pressure.

FAQ

Which is better: Safe, Squads or Fordefi?

Safe fits EVM smart-account multisig, Squads fits Solana treasury governance, and Fordefi fits institutional MPC operations across DeFi, CeFi and multiple chains.

What is the difference between multisig and MPC?

Multisig commonly enforces multiple onchain signatures through a smart contract, while MPC distributes cryptographic signing across parties without reconstructing a complete private key.

Which is best for Solana?

Squads is the natural starting point for Solana and SVM treasury controls. Buyers should verify current network and product support.

Which is best for EVM treasuries?

Safe is widely used for EVM smart-account multisig and onchain treasury governance. The setup still requires secure owners, tested modules and recovery procedures.

Which is best for institutional trading?

Fordefi is designed for institutional MPC policy and connectivity across DeFi and centralized venues, making it a natural trading-firm candidate.

Does multisig eliminate key risk?

No. Owners can still be compromised, collude or lose access. Contract modules, frontends and recovery processes also create risk.

What should treasury teams test?

Test policy changes, owner replacement, failed approvals, malicious transactions, DeFi interactions, recovery, vendor outage and complete audit export.

Is pricing public?

Safe and Squads offer products with different public and commercial tiers, while institutional Fordefi pricing is generally scoped. Confirm current prices and inclusions directly.
