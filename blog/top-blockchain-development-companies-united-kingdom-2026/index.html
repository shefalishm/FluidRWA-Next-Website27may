Where does each company fit?

Applied Blockchain: enterprise and privacy work

Applied Blockchain states it was founded in London, maintains its headquarters there and a development hub in Porto, and describes blockchain services. For a buyer, the important question is not the office label but who designs, builds, reviews and operates each component. Ask for a project-specific architecture showing where confidential data is held, how ledger state is verified, and who can change the deployed system.

Soken: development with security and legal workstreams

Soken publishes a London address and lists Web3 development, smart-contract audits and legal structuring. That combination may be relevant when a token project has multiple workstreams. Do not assume one contract covers all of them. Ask which legal jurisdiction is in scope, whether a security review is independent of the development team, and what report or opinion will be delivered.

Ethereal Labs: on-chain product engineering

Ethereal Labs describes itself as a London Web3 studio building smart contracts and full-stack applications. For a launch-focused brief, request a walk-through of contract testing, upgrade controls, wallet flows, monitoring and incident procedures. Vendor-reported project numbers should not substitute for evidence from the proposed team.

Five questions a UK buyer should settle before contracting

  1. Who is the contracting entity? Confirm the entity signing the statement of work and where engineers and subcontractors work.
  2. Which decisions need independent advice? Separate technical design from legal characterisation, financial promotion and compliance judgments.
  3. How are security duties split? The developer, independent auditor and operating team should have explicit scopes and handoffs.
  4. What is the production definition of done? Require tests, documentation, monitoring, access handover and an incident runbook, not just deployed contracts.
  5. What happens after launch? Agree on maintenance, chain upgrades, vulnerability response and repository access.

Buyer checklist for tokenization projects

Ask every shortlisted firm to show how investor onboarding, permissions, asset servicing, custody and redemption connect to the contracts. A token-minting demonstration alone is not a functioning asset platform. The blockchain development directory provides a broader set of firms; this UK page is only a documented starting cohort.

FAQ

Which UK blockchain development company should an enterprise contact?

Match the brief first: enterprise integration, audit and legal coordination, or on-chain application engineering. Then ask for specific evidence from the proposed delivery team.

Are all three engineering teams entirely UK-based?

Not necessarily. For example, Applied Blockchain describes a London headquarters and a Porto development hub. Check each proposal's actual staffing and contracting arrangement.

Does a company offering legal structuring replace specialist UK legal advice?

No. Establish the scope and jurisdiction of any legal work, the responsible professional and how independent advice will be obtained when required.
