The board has approved the allocation. The assets have arrived. Then the first urgent withdrawal lands on a weekend, an approver is unavailable and the destination address has changed. This is the moment a custody decision stops being a presentation and becomes an operating system for trust.

A good custody arrangement should explain who can act, who owes the assets, how an instruction is checked and what happens when something fails. A long list of supported coins cannot answer those questions. Neither can an insurance headline or a familiar banking logo.

This guide compares institutional custody services and wallet infrastructure without treating them as interchangeable. Start with the table, narrow by the responsibility you are buying, then run the same acceptance exercises with each finalist. For a browseable directory rather than a procurement guide, use crypto custody providers.

Choose the custody model before choosing the company

Three questions do most of the early work: who owes safekeeping duties, who can sign, and who completes a trade? Write the answers separately even if one group proposes to supply everything.

A custody-service relationship

A contracted custodian accepts specified safekeeping and administration responsibilities. The relevant agreement should identify the entity, client assets, recordkeeping, instruction process and return of assets. Ask what legal and operational treatment applies if the entity becomes insolvent or loses access to a subcontractor.

The phrase qualified custodian has a particular legal context; it is not a worldwide security rating. Ask your advisers which requirements apply to the institution, mandate and assets. A marketing statement that a group offers qualified custody does not establish that the proposed entity or product satisfies your own obligations.

Self-custody technology

Wallet software can provide signing infrastructure, approval policies, automation and integration without taking the same safekeeping role. Your organization may retain responsibility for administrators, recovery material, transaction policy and continuity. That can be appropriate when it is an intentional operating choice with named owners.

Fireblocks illustrates why the product matters: its self-custody infrastructure and Fireblocks Trust Company are distinct routes. Taurus also separates technology products from financial services. Never infer the legal role from the name of the software on an operator's screen.

Custody connected to prime or settlement services

A prime relationship may connect custody, execution, collateral and reporting. Convenience does not remove the need to identify where an asset sits at each stage. A custody balance, trading balance and pledged collateral position may carry different restrictions and counterparties.

Ask for a movement diagram from deposit through trade, settlement and withdrawal. Mark every change of entity, account, authority or encumbrance. If the diagram stops at an API call rather than the final reconciled asset and cash records, the operating model is unfinished.

Provider differences that matter in a real procurement

Anchorage Digital and BitGo: start with the entity and wallet arrangement

Anchorage Digital describes institutional custody alongside related services. The group also describes a self-custody route, so do not transfer promises between products. Ask whether the proposed account provides custody only or adds trading, staking, fiat or settlement, and which agreement governs each activity.

BitGo publishes an entity-by-service matrix that distinguishes regional entities from self-custody offerings. That is useful because a global brand does not supply one identical legal product everywhere. Compare the proposed row and wallet type against the actual assets, trading needs and recovery procedures in your brief.

For either provider, make a withdrawal under the proposed policy and request the statement that records it. Marketing access described as always on is not the same as an unconditional final-settlement deadline.

Coinbase Prime and Fidelity Digital Assets: connect custody to execution carefully

Coinbase's custody page identifies Coinbase Custody Trust Company and describes Vault storage and SOC 1 Type II and SOC 2 Type II audits. Read the agreement alongside the Prime workflow: which balances are held in custody, which are available for trading and what happens when assets move between those states?

Fidelity Digital Assets describes an integrated institutional custody and trading platform, including physical, cyber and digital controls. Ask for supported assets and activities for the proposed account rather than carrying over the product list from an individual-investor service with a similar brand name.

For both, require trade identifiers, asset movements, statements and administrator exports to reconcile. Integrated execution is useful only when operations can explain a pending or failed trade without guessing from a dashboard balance.

Fireblocks, Fordefi and Utila: define the operating responsibility

Fireblocks provides self-custody infrastructure and a separately described Trust Company offering. Keep them as distinct lines in the evaluation. If purchasing software, identify your signing participants, policy owners and recovery dependencies. If purchasing custody, obtain the relevant service agreement and asset schedule.

Fordefi describes institutional MPC wallets and DeFi controls. A transaction simulation can help operators understand a proposed interaction, but it is not a guarantee against every contract, oracle or protocol failure. Test permissions, spending approvals and a transaction involving an unfamiliar contract before approving a DeFi workflow.

Utila describes wallet infrastructure, treasury, stablecoin payments and business-continuity capabilities. Ask how an authorized transfer completes if its usual interface or a required service is unavailable. Treat recovery and API access as tested processes, not assumptions that follow from the term self-custody.

Copper and Komainu: evaluate collateral rights, not just connectivity

Copper's ClearLoop materials describe a network connecting custody, trading venues and collateral infrastructure. Komainu describes custody and partner connectivity through Komainu Connect. Those service directions make them relevant where an institution wants to deploy collateral without treating every trading venue as the long-term storage location.

Ask what assets are pledged, whether positions are netted, who may instruct release and how disputes are resolved. Identify the entity facing the venue and any custodian or intermediary that can block the transaction. A reduced need to prefund an exchange does not establish that every counterparty exposure has disappeared.

BNY, Paxos and Sygnum: separate adjacent financial services

BNY presents digital asset custody alongside institutional asset servicing. An existing traditional custody relationship can be an operational starting point, but ask for the digital asset service schedule, eligibility and reporting scope. A familiar account relationship does not mean every token can be deposited.

Paxos describes enterprise infrastructure covering custody, brokerage and issuance. An issuer choosing infrastructure for its product should distinguish custody of customer tokens from custody of stablecoin reserves or other underlying assets. Different entities and contracts may perform those jobs.

Sygnum describes custody within a digital asset banking offering, with related trading, payments and settlement services. Confirm the booking entity and permitted client relationship. Do not assume that a banking or payment feature is included in a custody-only mandate.

Gemini, Hex Trust and Zodia Custody: verify the current workflow

Gemini's current custody materials describe MPC signing, unique digital addresses, portal and API access, and approval requirements for address changes and withdrawals. Older comparisons describing only historical cold-storage architecture can miss this product detail. Request the controls that apply to the account being opened today.

Hex Trust markets custody alongside payments, global markets and wealth services. Ask which local entity supplies each activity. Zodia Custody describes institutional custody with segregated wallets and configurable operating workflows. For both, test the specific withdrawal sequence, including compliance holds and unusual destinations, rather than equating advertised speed with unconditional availability.

Taurus: deployment and service contracts are separate choices

Taurus-PROTECT describes hot, warm and cold custody infrastructure, HSM and MPC capabilities, and managed, on-premise or hybrid deployment. Taurus-CAPITAL and Taurus-NETWORK address different tasks in the stack. Buying one module does not appoint every operator needed for a tokenized asset business.

Ask who controls keys and backups in the selected deployment, who maintains the infrastructure and which financial-service agreement, if any, appoints a safekeeping provider. Evaluate software sovereignty, hosting and the legal custody role separately.

How to check regulation without trusting a badge

Record the contracting entity's full name, register identifier, regulator, territory, permitted activities and check date. Match these to the actual service, not the parent company. Keep the result with the agreement and ask counsel to assess its relevance to your mandate.

For US entities, use current regulator records and distinguish an application or conditional decision from operational status. The OCC's December 12, 2025 announcement explicitly describes conditional approvals. That dated announcement alone is not a current operating-entity check.

For EU crypto-asset services, consult ESMA's MiCA register and the relevant national authority. Check custody and administration as a service rather than assuming another listed activity covers it. Financial instruments and other asset types require their own scope assessment.

For the UK, the FCA's cryptoasset AML/CTF guidance explains that registration is not an endorsement and does not itself provide Financial Ombudsman Service or FSCS protection. Avoid turning a registration into a blanket quality or investor-protection claim.

A concrete example of activity-specific checking is the AMF entry for Zodia Custody (Europe) S.A.. It identifies a Luxembourg entity and custody/administration and transfer activities for cross-border services in France. It is not a permission statement about every Zodia group entity worldwide.

Security architecture: compare what can actually fail

MPC, multisig, HSM and cold storage describe different aspects of a system. They are not four competing quality grades. MPC distributes signing computation; multisig requires multiple signatures under a defined arrangement; an HSM protects cryptographic operations in hardware; cold storage concerns exposure and operating design.

Ask where authority can concentrate despite the architecture. Can one administrator replace approvers, change a beneficiary and release a transaction? Does a vendor-operated co-signer have to remain available? Can a cloud administrator access several supposedly independent components? What happens if a recovery operator leaves?

Segregation, insurance and proof of reserves answer different questions

On-chain segregation identifies an address arrangement. Legal segregation concerns rights and treatment under the agreement and applicable law. Operational segregation concerns records, access and day-to-day handling. Ask about all three. An omnibus wallet is not automatically evidence of missing client records, and a unique address alone is not a legal opinion on insolvency.

Proof of reserves can show a defined asset observation, but it does not necessarily show all liabilities, encumbrances, off-chain claims or the ownership rights of a particular customer. Ask for the methodology, scope and observation time. Reconcile it with statements and contractual rights rather than treating it as a substitute for them.

For insurance, ask who is insured, which assets and wallets are covered, which events trigger coverage and who submits the claim. Establish whether limits are aggregate or client-specific and whether a loss has to meet a defined theft or key-loss condition. Do not treat insurance as protection against market losses or every smart-contract incident.

Run three examples with the provider: an external key theft, an authorized instruction induced by fraud and a protocol failure after assets enter DeFi. Ask for the relevant wording, not a yes/no answer that assets are insured. The custody insurance diligence guide expands the evidence request.

For staking, identify validator selection, delegation authority, reward accounting, slashing exposure, unbonding and exit conditions. Do not compare advertised yields as if they guarantee the same liquidity or risk. A custody account supporting an asset does not automatically support staking it.

For tokenized assets, compare the legal ownership register, token balance and custodian statement. Identify who can freeze, recover, replace or cancel the token. Custody of a fund token does not establish custody of the fund's underlying securities, property or cash. Keep these roles separate in the custody, wallet and settlement role guide.

Settlement: test both legs and the failure path

A transaction broadcast is not the same as a reconciled settlement. Ask what proves the asset leg is final, what proves the cash leg is final and which party can release each. Define the treatment of a chain reorganization, screening hold, failed bank payment and disputed trade.

In a fictional purchase, the asset has moved but the cash instruction is delayed. The useful answer identifies the exposure owner, escalation route and authorized correction. It does not simply promise that the integration is fast.

  1. Approve the counterparty and destination under independent controls.
  2. Identify available, pledged and restricted balances separately.
  3. Authorize each instruction using its own reference and policy.
  4. Track submitted, approved, broadcast, confirmed and settled states.
  5. Reconcile both legs to the trade and custody records.
  6. Hold an incomplete instruction for investigation rather than blindly resubmitting it.
  7. Record the authorized correction, final state and evidence used to close the exception.

For off-exchange arrangements, add collateral valuation, haircuts, netting, settlement frequency, default, dispute and release terms. Ask whether rights change if a venue, custodian or technology operator fails. Do not label two unrelated transfers delivery-versus-payment just because they occur close together.

Integration and reporting: make the accountant part of the demo

Operations should be able to reconstruct a holding without relying on one dashboard screenshot. Request a sample balance statement, transaction history, approval log and exception report. Match account and wallet identifiers to transaction hashes, internal references, timestamps, asset units and final status.

Test a missed webhook. Your system should recover through a documented query or reconciliation process, not create a second withdrawal because an event was not received. Confirm idempotency behaviour, pagination, rate limits, event ordering, retry policy, timezone handling and historical export availability in the actual API documentation.

Keep a distinction between available balance, total balance, pledged collateral, unsettled trades and staked assets. Define how network charges, staking rewards and corrections appear in the ledger. Ask the administrator to reproduce a month-end position using exported records, including an incomplete transaction and a reversal or correction where supported.

For a bank, add booking-centre separation and core-system interfaces. For a payment business, add payment references, compliance holds and beneficiary exceptions. For a fund, add administrator access and reporting cutoffs. The best integration is the one your team can explain under audit, not the one with the longest partner list.

A migration plan that leaves an exit open

Start with an approved asset inventory and named business owners. Identify legal entities, account relationships, counterparties, administrators and signers. Separate transferable assets from pledged, staked, restricted or suspended positions before setting a migration date.

Use a small approved test amount and verify the destination out of band. Complete a deposit, withdrawal and statement reconciliation. Move in controlled batches only after the expected records agree. Pause on an unexplained difference; do not use a successful first transfer as permission to ignore the remaining exceptions.

Do not assume key export is possible or appropriate for a custody-service transition. Some migrations require an authorized asset transfer rather than export of key material. For self-custody infrastructure, determine exactly what recovery material can be used, by whom and under what dependencies.

Rehearse an exit before the contract becomes operationally indispensable. Retain records in a usable format and agree who supports a transition if the provider stops accepting an asset. A second custodian can improve options, but it also creates another system to secure and reconcile; redundancy is a design decision, not an automatic benefit.

A practical scorecard and five go-live tests

Use mandatory gates before a weighted score. An unresolved service permission, ownership treatment or recovery dependency should not be offset by a beautiful interface. The following is a proposed buyer framework, not published provider ratings.

DimensionSuggested weightEvidence
Service and mandate fit25%Entity, activities, asset schedule and agreed responsibilities
Asset protection and control25%Segregation, signing, approvals and recovery
Operational resilience20%Incident handling, continuity and withdrawal exceptions
Settlement and reporting20%Counterparty model, reconciliation and administrator records
Integration and portability10%API behaviour, data exports and exit rehearsal

Give each evidence item a named reviewer. Distinguish a document received, a question answered and a test passed. A missing policy or incomplete test should stay open in the decision record rather than becoming a positive score because the provider is well known.

Before go-live, complete five exercises with the responsible teams present:

  1. Changed destination: independently validate a new address and prove that one user cannot bypass the policy.
  2. Unavailable signer: replace or recover access without giving one person unrestricted authority.
  3. Delayed settlement: identify the incomplete leg and reconcile the final correction.
  4. Missed event: recover an API or webhook gap without duplicating a transaction.
  5. Exit: export the required records and complete an authorized limited transfer to the replacement arrangement.

FAQ

What are the best institutional crypto custody providers in 2026?

There is no universal winner. Anchorage Digital, BitGo, Coinbase Prime, Fidelity Digital Assets and Gemini are custody-service starting points; Copper and Komainu also describe collateral connectivity. Compare the contracted entity, eligible assets, segregation, withdrawal controls and reporting. Treat wallet technology and a separately contracted custody service as different purchases.

Is Fireblocks a custodian or wallet technology provider?

Fireblocks offers self-custody infrastructure and separately describes custody through Fireblocks Trust Company. Establish which product and entity the proposal covers. Do not apply the Trust Company service description to every Fireblocks wallet customer.

Does MPC mean assets are held by a qualified custodian?

No. MPC describes a signing architecture, not a legal appointment. Determine who owes safekeeping duties, who can authorize transactions, which entity contracts with you and which requirements apply to your mandate.

Does crypto custody insurance protect every customer loss?

No. Coverage depends on insured parties, covered wallets, events, exclusions, limits and claims conditions. Obtain the relevant policy evidence and ask whether limits are shared. Market losses, protocol failures and authorized but fraudulent instructions need separate review.

Is off-exchange settlement the same as eliminating counterparty risk?

No. Keeping collateral away from a trading venue can change exposure, but the agreement still determines pledges, netting, release, default and close-out. Test a failed settlement, a disputed balance and a venue interruption before relying on the arrangement.

How should a fund check custody for tokenized assets?

Check the exact network, token contract, investor eligibility and transfer restrictions. Reconcile the legal ownership register, token balance and custodian statement. Agree who handles freezes, wallet replacement, distributions and redemption; holding a token does not mean holding its underlying property or reserve assets.

What should an institution test before moving assets to a new custodian?

Run a limited deposit and withdrawal, a changed-destination approval, an unavailable-signer recovery, a missed-webhook reconciliation and a final data export. Use a small approved scope and stop on unexplained differences before migrating the remaining assets.

How do you check whether a crypto custodian is regulated?

Match the exact legal entity to the relevant regulator's current register, activity scope and territory. An application, conditional approval or AML registration is not interchangeable with permission for every custody service. Confirm the agreement and your institution's obligations with appropriate advisers.
