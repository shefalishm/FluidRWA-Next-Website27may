iCapital vs VerifyInvestor vs InvestReady: Investor Checks

Compare investor identity and accreditation hand-offs for US offerings, including where current 506(c) scope needs confirmation.

Reviewed by FluidRWA Research Team · October 15, 2026

Short answer

iCapital Identity Solutions currently describes reusable identity and AML/KYC services; VerifyInvestor and InvestReady describe accredited-investor verification. They are not interchangeable services. For a US Rule 506(c) offering, confirm each provider's current accreditation scope and how its result, evidence and expiry enter the issuer's workflow.

Which accreditation workflow fits a tokenized securities offering?

Start with the offering's exemption and investor population, not a provider logo. The SEC's Rule 506(c) guidance says issuers must take reasonable steps to verify accredited-investor status. The rule is a US securities-law context; this article is a workflow comparison, not a determination that a specific token or offering qualifies.

The cohort spans two accreditation specialists and one broader identity/onboarding service. It is not a like-for-like ranking or an exhaustive market list. iCapital's current public page documents identity and AML/KYC services; its current 506(c) accreditation scope was not verified in this review. Official product pages establish what each company says it offers; they do not independently validate a customer's legal compliance or conversion rate.

iCapital Identity Solutions: test reuse without losing context

iCapital announced its acquisition of Parallel Markets in January 2025. Its current Identity Solutions page describes reusable investor identity records and AML/KYC review. The older Parallel Markets accreditation URL now redirects to that broader page, so this review does not treat the historic accreditation description as proof of a currently contracted 506(c) service. Ask iCapital directly for the legal entity, verification method and deliverable offered today.

For a platform offering more than one product, the practical question is whether a prior verification can be used in the new offering, with what date and evidence, and who approves an exception. Reuse can reduce duplicated collection, but it cannot substitute for a current, offering-specific compliance judgment. Request the record format and test an investor whose status changes after the original verification.

VerifyInvestor: test the verification-to-issuer hand-off

VerifyInvestor describes accredited-investor verification and other investor-status services; its API materials describe an integration path. Pilot a real review sequence with synthetic data: invite, document collection, result, issuer notification, exception and renewal. Check whether the proposed contract includes exactly the evidence and qualified-purchaser or identity checks your workflow needs. Their coexistence on a marketing page is not proof that every check is bundled.

InvestReady: test completion and evidence retrieval

InvestReady describes an investor accreditation flow. Its consulting materials also discuss tokenization-related requirements. The buyer should test the applicant's path on mobile, resubmission after an incomplete file and how staff retrieve a result at subscription time. Do not infer that a verification provider is your transfer agent, broker-dealer or securities counsel.

Run the same pilot for all three

Use a synthetic accredited individual and an entity with layered beneficial ownership. Record what the investor sees, who requests documents, how a reviewer rejects or requests more, and exactly what artifact reaches the issuer. Then simulate a material change before a second offering and ask whether the platform can identify stale evidence.

FAQ

Is investor self-certification enough for a Rule 506(c) offering?

The SEC says issuers must take reasonable steps to verify accredited-investor status in a Rule 506(c) offering. The appropriate method depends on the facts and requires legal review; a checked box alone is not the workflow compared here.

Does an accreditation result also complete KYC or AML?

Not automatically. Check the contracted product and evidence for each required control; accreditation, identity, sanctions and suitability are separate decisions.

Can a verification be reused indefinitely?

No universal reuse period is established by this comparison. Ask each provider about evidence date, renewal, change events and the issuer's offering-specific obligations.
