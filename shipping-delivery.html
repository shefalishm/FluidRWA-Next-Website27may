FluidRWA Policies

Digital service delivery

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Digital services only

FluidRWA provides vendor vetting reviews and separately agreed marketing, visibility and advertising services. These are digital services. No physical goods are sold or shipped.

Vetting review

There is a fee for the vetting review. To begin, apply to be a Vetted Vendor and provide your company details and supporting evidence. We may contact you by email to ask for missing information or clarify a claim.

We communicate the review outcome by email. A Vetted badge is awarded only after the review is completed and approved. Paying the review fee does not guarantee approval.

Marketing, visibility and advertising

Marketing, visibility and advertising have separate fees. The scope, required materials, delivery method and schedule are agreed separately. These services do not determine the vetting decision.

Delivery confirmation

Delivery is confirmed by email or by completion of the agreed digital service, such as an approved profile update, campaign asset or visibility placement. The confirmation depends on the service agreed with you.

Delays

Incomplete information, missing assets, verification requirements, customer-requested changes or third-party platform dependencies may affect delivery. We will contact you if further information is needed. For support, email contact@fluidrwa.com.

Refunds and cancellation

Fees are generally non-refundable. If your company does not pass the vetting review, the review-cost deduction and remaining-balance refund follow our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
