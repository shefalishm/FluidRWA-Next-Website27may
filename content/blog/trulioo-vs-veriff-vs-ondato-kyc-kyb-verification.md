## The Short Answer

Trulioo, Veriff and Ondato overlap, but they are optimized around different buying priorities.

**Choose Trulioo** when global person and business coverage is the central challenge. Its platform brings electronic identity verification, document verification and business verification into a normalized API model.

**Choose Veriff** when the core requirement is a polished document-and-selfie identity workflow, biometric or database verification, and flexibility across hosted interfaces, SDKs and direct API integration.

**Choose Ondato** when the project wants a broader compliance operating suite combining customer and business onboarding, AML screening, case management and ongoing controls.

The decision should not be based on a generic country count. It should be based on the **specific checks, registries, documents, evidence and exception workflow** needed for each customer segment.

## KYC Is a Control System, Not a Widget

A tokenization platform, stablecoin business or Web3 financial application does not merely need to capture an identity document. It needs to make and defend a risk decision.

A complete onboarding flow may require:

- person data and document verification
- selfie, liveness or face matching
- proof of address
- sanctions and politically exposed person screening
- adverse-media review
- business-registry verification
- ownership and beneficial-owner identification
- verification of directors and authorized representatives
- source-of-funds or accreditation evidence
- manual review and escalation
- ongoing screening after onboarding
- an audit trail explaining the decision

No vendor removes the regulated business’s responsibility to design this policy. The provider supplies data, workflows and evidence; the customer determines the legal basis, risk rules and final acceptance decision.

## Provider Profiles

### Trulioo

Trulioo’s developer documentation presents three main offerings: electronic person verification, document verification and business verification. Its normalized platform is designed to abstract differences among data sources and jurisdictions behind a consistent integration.

That breadth makes Trulioo especially relevant to companies expanding across markets or onboarding both individuals and legal entities. The same program may need consumer identity verification in one country, business-registry data in another and document fallback where electronic data is insufficient.

Trulioo is a strong fit when:

- global KYC and KYB coverage is more important than a single capture experience
- the business onboards companies as well as individuals
- developers want normalized person and business verification APIs
- multiple data sources and local registries must be orchestrated
- the compliance team expects geographic expansion
- a single vendor relationship across several verification modes is valuable

Its main limitation is the same challenge faced by every broad platform: “supported” does not mean an identical product in each country. Buyers must map exact fields, sources, match logic, document support, fallback behavior and response evidence market by market.

### Veriff

Veriff is strongly centered on identity-verification sessions. A buyer creates a session, sends or captures user data and media, submits the session and receives decisions through webhooks or API retrieval. Its product documentation covers document and selfie verification, document-only verification, biometric verification, age estimation, proof of address, AML screening and registry checks.

The implementation choice is flexible. Teams can redirect users to a hosted flow, use web or native SDKs, or build their own front end and interact directly with the API. That makes Veriff a natural fit for product teams that care deeply about the user journey and want control over how verification is embedded.

Veriff is a strong fit when:

- document authenticity and selfie or biometric checks are central
- the product needs web and mobile capture options
- developers want session-based APIs and webhooks
- a hosted flow is useful for fast implementation
- the team may later move to a more customized front end
- database verification or proof-of-address checks are part of the plan

Veriff is less obviously a first-choice replacement for a deep global KYB platform. Business onboarding may require another service or a broader compliance stack depending on the jurisdictions and ownership complexity involved.

### Ondato

Ondato positions itself as a compliance platform rather than only an identity-check endpoint. Its offering spans KYC, KYB, AML screening, case and risk management, and ongoing compliance workflows.

That breadth is useful for companies that do not want to stitch together an identity vendor, business-verification vendor, screening tool and internal case system. It is particularly relevant when compliance operations need to review exceptions, assign cases and monitor customers after the initial decision.

Ondato is a strong fit when:

- the buyer wants KYC and KYB in one operating environment
- sanctions, PEP and adverse-media screening are part of onboarding
- case management and manual review matter
- ongoing monitoring is required
- the team values an integrated compliance workflow over best-of-breed components
- internal compliance users need an interface as much as developers need an API

The diligence question is depth by module. A broad suite should be tested to confirm that its document coverage, business registries, screening sources, workflow flexibility and API behavior are each sufficient. Consolidation is useful only if the underlying controls meet the required standard.

## The Most Important Comparison: Evidence by Market

Instead of asking “Do you cover Germany?” ask:

1. Which German identity documents are supported?
2. Is NFC used or available for compatible documents?
3. Which government or credit databases are queried?
4. How are names, addresses and dates normalized?
5. What result is returned for partial matches?
6. Which business registries and corporate forms are covered?
7. How many ownership layers can be resolved?
8. Is the source and timestamp included in the audit evidence?
9. What sends the case to manual review?
10. How quickly are data-source changes reflected?

Repeat the exercise for every material market. A ten-country launch can require ten different control paths.

## Fit by Use Case

### Global consumer or investor onboarding

Compare Trulioo and Veriff first. Trulioo is attractive when electronic data coverage and cross-market normalization are crucial. Veriff is attractive when document capture, liveness and conversion are the primary design problem. A layered workflow may use database verification first and document verification as a fallback.

### Business and institutional onboarding

Compare Trulioo and Ondato first. Trulioo offers dedicated business verification and broad entity coverage. Ondato may reduce operational fragmentation when KYB, owner checks, screening and case management should live in one suite.

Institutional onboarding also needs fields that a consumer KYC flow may omit:

- incorporation and good-standing status
- registered and operating addresses
- directors and authorized signers
- ownership percentages and control persons
- nested legal entities
- trust or fund structures
- sanctions exposure of entities and individuals
- supporting documents and expiry dates

### Crypto exchange or wallet onboarding

All three may be relevant, but identity verification is only one control. The final architecture must connect onboarding risk to wallet screening, transaction monitoring, Travel Rule processes, withdrawal controls and ongoing customer review.

### Tokenized securities or private funds

The product may need identity, KYB, investor qualification, jurisdictional eligibility, tax documentation and transfer restrictions. Verify that the provider’s result can feed the tokenization platform and transfer-control system without losing the evidence behind the decision.

## Conversion vs Risk: Do Not Optimize the Wrong Metric

KYC vendors often discuss completion and pass rates. Those are useful, but they can mislead.

A high approval rate can mean excellent local data coverage. It can also mean lenient matching. A low approval rate can reveal weak coverage, poor capture design or a genuinely risky population.

Track the whole funnel:

- start-to-completion rate
- document-capture retries
- automated approval rate
- false rejection rate
- confirmed fraud that passed
- manual-review rate and handle time
- inconclusive or abandoned sessions
- average and p95 decision time
- webhook failures and duplicate events
- support tickets per thousand checks

Risk and conversion should be segmented by country, document, device, customer type and acquisition channel. An aggregate pass rate hides the exact markets that need redesign.

## Data Protection and Procurement Checklist

Identity systems process high-risk personal data. Ask:

- Which entity is controller and which is processor?
- Where are images, biometrics and extracted fields stored?
- Which subprocessors and data sources are involved?
- What retention is configurable?
- How are deletion and data-subject requests handled?
- Is biometric data created, and under which legal basis?
- Can production staff access customer media?
- How are API secrets, webhooks and dashboards secured?
- What certifications and independent assurance reports are available?
- What happens to data when the contract ends?
- Can the vendor support regulatory examinations and evidence requests?

The answer must be documented in contracts and architecture, not inferred from a security badge.

## Proof-of-Concept Method

Build a consented test set across the top launch markets. Include:

- common and less common valid documents
- older documents and names with transliteration
- poor lighting and low-spec devices
- partial database matches
- legal entities with simple and layered ownership
- cases that should trigger screening or manual review
- interrupted sessions and webhook retries

Do not use synthetic “perfect” users only. The purpose is to expose operational friction and evidence gaps before launch.

Score each provider on accuracy, completion, manual review, integration effort, audit evidence, KYB depth, compliance-user experience, support and total cost per successfully onboarded customer.

## Final Verdict

- Choose **Trulioo** for broad global KYC and KYB coverage through a normalized platform.
- Choose **Veriff** for embedded document, selfie, biometric and database identity verification.
- Choose **Ondato** for consolidated KYC, KYB, AML and ongoing compliance operations.

For many regulated businesses, the final design may combine strengths: one provider for global electronic checks, another for high-assurance document capture, and a separate transaction-monitoring layer. The best stack is the one that produces a defensible decision with acceptable user friction in every target market.
