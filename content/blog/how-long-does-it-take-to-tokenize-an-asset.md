## Build a schedule around dependencies

The launch date is usually determined by the slowest unresolved dependency, not by how quickly someone can deploy a token. Map the legal instrument, asset eligibility, target investors, payment path and servicing arrangements before asking a provider for a timeline. Record dependencies with an owner and an acceptance test.

| Milestone | Evidence that it is ready | What commonly blocks it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Define what the investor holds | Counsel and issuer agree on the instrument, rights and investor scope | Unresolved asset title, issuer structure or market eligibility |
| Choose providers | Responsible entities and responsibilities are written down | Platform, custodian, administrator or payment provider scopes don't line up |
| Configure and integrate | A test subscription reaches the ownership record with traceable payment evidence | Missing data mappings, partner access or approval procedures |
| Rehearse exceptions | Staff can handle a rejected investor, payment mismatch and restricted transfer | Unclear ownership of repairs or incomplete reconciliation |
| Launch and service | Issuer signs off on the operating model and first reporting cycle | No operating owner for distributions, transfers or escalations |

## What can happen in parallel?

While counsel reviews terms, the operations team can draw the subscription flow and prepare synthetic test data. Engineering can test interfaces without accepting real funds. Do not lock production settings that depend on unresolved legal or operating decisions. A pilot with one asset and a limited investor cohort may reduce integration work, but it does not replace applicable approvals.

## Example: an existing fund adds a tokenized share class

The team may already have a fund administrator and investor process. Its critical question is where the definitive holdings are recorded. Start with the document or agreement that designates that record, then test three events: a funded subscription, a rejected transfer and reconciliation of the end-of-day balance. If the platform can issue units but the administrator cannot reconcile them, the setup is still in testing.

Request a provider estimate that separates configuration from buyer-owned and third-party dependencies. Ask which milestones are assumed complete at kickoff and how the schedule moves if an approval is delayed. Do not publish a universal launch duration from one provider quote.
