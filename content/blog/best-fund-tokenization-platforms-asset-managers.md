## Fund Tokenization Platforms: Quick Answer

The best fund tokenization platform is the one that fits the fund's legal and operational structure. Asset managers should compare the full workflow, not just token issuance.

| Platform | Natural starting point | Confirm before selection |
|---|---|---|
| Securitize | Regulated US digital-securities and fund workflows | Entity roles, fund eligibility, transfer agency and distribution |
| Tokeny | ERC-3643 permissioned token infrastructure | Administrator integrations, identity controls and operating ownership |
| Zoniqx | Modular issuance, identity, lifecycle and distribution tooling | Licensed intermediaries, production evidence and supported modules |
| Taurus | Bank-oriented custody, tokenization and asset servicing infrastructure | Product modules, custody structure, chain support and implementation |
| KAIO (formerly Libre) | Tokenized fund issuance and distribution | Eligible investors, administrator dependencies and redemption process |

This is an editorial shortlist, not a ranking or endorsement. Capabilities and availability change. Obtain current proposals and legal advice for the intended fund and jurisdictions.

## Start with the Fund Operating Model

Before comparing software, open [FluidRWA Fund Comparison](/network-explorer/fund-comparison?funds=fobxx,ustb,uscc) to inspect source-linked appointments for three documented fund models. For investor access and sales responsibilities, see [how tokenized funds are distributed](/blog/tokenized-fund-distribution-models). A named transfer agent does not establish a distribution mandate.

Before scoring software, make a responsibility matrix for the transfer agent, administrator, custodian and distribution partner. For each role record the legal entity, authoritative record and fallback if it cannot process a subscription or redemption. The [October Network Explorer findings](/network-explorer/research/tokenized-fund-infrastructure-october-2026#october-findings) give examples of disclosed role assignments; use them to frame questions, not to infer coverage for your fund.

[Share your fund requirements](/submit-requirement?source=fund-article-buyer) with the domicile, investor type and operating roles still open.

Before requesting demonstrations, document:

- fund domicile, legal form and offering exemptions
- investor types and target jurisdictions
- authoritative ownership record and transfer-agent responsibilities
- administrator, NAV and accounting workflow
- subscription, capital-call, distribution and redemption process
- custody model for fund interests, cash and digital assets
- wallet model and investor recovery process
- primary distribution and permitted secondary transfers

A platform can automate an agreed operating model. It cannot resolve an unclear legal structure or assign regulated responsibilities on the asset manager's behalf.

## Where Each Platform May Fit

### Securitize

Securitize combines tokenization technology with identified regulated functions across its group. It is a natural first investigation for established managers seeking a US-oriented digital-securities workflow. Buyers should separate each entity and agreement, then confirm asset eligibility, transfer-agent scope, custody connectivity and distribution.

### Tokeny

Tokeny centers its infrastructure on ERC-3643 and identity-linked permissioned transfers. It may fit banks, administrators and asset managers building modular EVM-based fund infrastructure. Confirm who operates identity registries, compliance rules, recovery, servicing and investor support.

### Zoniqx

Zoniqx presents modular tools spanning issuance, identity, lifecycle and distribution. It may fit managers seeking multi-chain flexibility, but buyers should verify production references, licensed-party dependencies and which modules are generally available. Its ERC-7518-related positioning should be read with the proposal's current Review status in mind.

### Taurus

Taurus is relevant to banks and regulated financial institutions evaluating custody, tokenization and digital asset operations in one infrastructure relationship. Confirm which modules, entities, networks and service responsibilities apply to the proposed fund.

### KAIO (formerly Libre)

KAIO (formerly Libre) is relevant to asset managers considering tokenized fund issuance and distribution to eligible investors. Confirm fund eligibility, administrator and custodian relationships, transfer restrictions, investor access and redemption mechanics.

## Proof-of-Concept Tests

1. Onboard one eligible and one ineligible investor.
2. Process a subscription through final reconciled ownership records.
3. Apply a lockup and reject an unauthorized transfer.
4. Update NAV and generate investor reporting.
5. Process a distribution with withholding and failed-payment handling.
6. Complete a redemption and reconcile token, cash and administrator records.
7. Recover an investor wallet without weakening identity controls.
8. Export the complete register, documents, rules and audit history.

## End-to-end fund launch: assign the work that software does not replace

The fund has an issuance platform, but who approves the NAV? Who keeps the shareholder register? Who accepts a subscription, holds the portfolio and pays a redemption? A platform comparison becomes useful when those questions are answered before the demo.

Use the [FluidRWA fund infrastructure evidence map](/network-explorer/research/tokenized-fund-infrastructure-october-2026) to inspect how named roles are recorded for specific products. The research retains its original reporting and observation dates. A named provider for one fund is not evidence that it performs the same role for every product using the same tokenization brand.

| Operating role | Evidence for procurement | Question for the platform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fund governance and management | Responsible entity and approved operating documents | Which actions require manager or board approval? |
| Custody or depositary | Actual appointment and eligible assets | How do portfolio and cash records enter the workflow? |
| Administration | NAV, accounting and investor servicing responsibilities | Which records are imported, approved and corrected? |
| Transfer agency or register | Authoritative holder record and change controls | How are wallet events reconciled to legal ownership? |
| Distribution and access | Relevant entities, investor eligibility and channel agreements | Who approves an investor and handles a rejected subscription? |

### Make redemption the deciding exercise

Use one fictional investor whose wallet balance, register holding and pending redemption can be traced together. Ask the team to show cutoffs, applicable fees, approval, token treatment, cash payment and the final register update.

Then delay the cash leg and change the investor's receiving account under the approved verification procedure. The system should distinguish a request, an approved obligation and a completed payment. Ask who investigates each discrepancy and who communicates with the investor.

### Ask for a complete launch and operating quote

Separate platform subscription, implementation, chain costs, administrator and transfer-agent fees, custody, investor onboarding, distribution and ongoing reporting. Confirm which services are supplied by the platform group and which require separate contracts.

For jurisdiction, define the fund domicile, manager, target investors, distribution locations and instrument. A platform available in a country is not an authorization for your fund or offering.

The [tokenization company shortlist](/blog/top-tokenization-companies-2026) helps separate software, access and market roles. ## An asset manager's launch gate: subscriptions must work in both directions

A fund launch can look complete when the first investor subscribes. The more revealing test is a rejected subscription followed by a redemption, a wallet change and a revised investor statement. The manager needs a coordinated operating model for all four, not simply a token that transfers successfully.

[Tokeny's documentation](https://docs.tokeny.com/docs/t-rex-platform) describes issuer servicing and investor applications. [Zoniqx](https://www.zoniqx.com/) separates z360 issuance and lifecycle workflows from zConnect distribution. These are useful starting points for a responsibility map; neither product label establishes who is appointed to administer your fund or accept its investors.

| Launch gate | What the manager must establish | Evidence to approve |
|---|---|---|
| Fund structure | Domicile, vehicle and investor rights | Approved offering documentation |
| Service appointments | Administrator, register owner and custodian | Named agreements and boundaries |
| Eligibility | Investor type and jurisdiction rules | Product-specific onboarding test |
| Subscription | Money receipt, cutoff and allocation | Reconciled instruction and ownership entry |
| Ownership record | Authority across register and chain | Exception and correction procedure |
| Distribution | Actual investor-access mandate | Scope, restrictions and remuneration terms |
| Redemption | Liquidity, notice and payment ownership | Full cancellation-to-payment rehearsal |
| Reporting | NAV, fees and investor statements | Approved source-to-statement trace |
| Recovery | Wallet replacement and operator continuity | Independently approved recovery test |
| Exit | Data and operational portability | Complete export and transition plan |

### Treat alternatives as different operating models

A team comparing Securitize, Tokeny, Zoniqx, Taurus and KAIO should explain why each candidate belongs in the same request for proposal. A manager seeking software for its existing appointed operators has a different purchase from a manager seeking access to a coordinated service stack. A distribution relationship is another distinct decision.

For a smaller private-asset offering, include DigiShares or another relevant issuer-software option from the [platform directory](/vendors/tokenization-platforms) only when the proposed scope fits. Do not add every provider to create a longer list. Compare a narrower set against the same obligations and operating records.

### Ten questions to settle before the implementation budget

1. Which entity owns the fund's authoritative investor record?
2. Who accepts or rejects a subscription?
3. What happens to unmatched or early money?
4. Who approves token issuance and cancellation?
5. How are NAV changes and fee adjustments propagated?
6. What proves an investor received a distribution?
7. Which party supplies actual distribution access?
8. What happens when an eligible investor loses a wallet?
9. Which launch dependencies are outside the platform contract?
10. Can the administrator service investors after a platform migration?
