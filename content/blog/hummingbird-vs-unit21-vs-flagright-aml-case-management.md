## Vendor profiles

### Hummingbird

Hummingbird describes a platform for case management, investigation and regulatory reporting across AML, customer diligence, disputes and compliance testing. It is designed to bring together data from monitoring, KYC and watchlist providers.

**Good fit:** Teams with an existing detection stack that need a consistent analyst workspace and reporting process.

**Potential limitation:** The value depends on data quality and integration. Confirm entity resolution, alert lineage, filing support by jurisdiction and export of the complete investigation record.

### Unit21

Unit21 offers fraud and AML infrastructure spanning transaction monitoring, rules, case management and related risk operations.

**Good fit:** Fintechs and payments teams that want detection and investigation configured within a common platform.

**Potential limitation:** Configurable rules still require ownership, testing and tuning. Separate vendor-reported performance from results on the buyer's population and typologies.

### Flagright

Flagright documents centralized case operations, QA, analyst metrics, SAR workflows and AI-assisted investigations within an AML and fraud platform.

**Good fit:** Teams seeking a modern integrated workflow with automation across alert investigation, narrative drafting and quality review.

**Potential limitation:** Require explainability, role separation, approval gates, version history and accuracy testing before relying on AI-generated investigation content.

## Best fit by buyer scenario

| Buyer scenario | Likely starting point | Reason to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Existing Chainalysis or KYC stack | Hummingbird | A provider-neutral workspace may consolidate alerts without replacing every source system. |
| Fast-growing fintech | Unit21 or Flagright | Compare detection breadth, workflow speed, implementation effort and analyst controls. |
| High SAR volume | Hummingbird or Flagright | Test jurisdiction templates, narrative evidence, review, filing acknowledgement and amendments. |
| Fraud and AML convergence | Unit21 or Flagright | A shared platform can reduce handoffs if privacy, permissions and typology ownership remain clear. |
| Regulated institution | Any after model-risk review | Audit trail, data retention, explainability, segregation and validated reporting matter more than headline automation. |

## Work one alert to closure

Give each provider the same alert, including a false positive and a case that needs escalation. Have an analyst collect evidence, assign a reviewer, record the decision and export the audit trail. Ask how rules are changed and how old cases can be revisited after a policy update. The number of integrations matters less if investigators cannot explain their decisions later.

## Conclusion

Choose Hummingbird when a provider-neutral investigation and regulatory-reporting workspace is the main requirement; Unit21 when transaction monitoring, fraud detection and case management should form a configurable no-code risk stack; and Flagright when an AI-native AML and fraud operations platform with integrated investigation workflows is preferred.
