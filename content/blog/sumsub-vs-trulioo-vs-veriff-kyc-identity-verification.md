| Natural buyer | Fintech, crypto or marketplace needing several risk controls in one operational environment | Global enterprise onboarding people and businesses across many markets | Digital platform optimizing remote user verification and fraud-resistant capture |

## Vendor profiles

### Sumsub

Sumsub presents a full-cycle verification platform combining identity and business verification with AML, transaction monitoring, fraud prevention and case management. Its identity documentation describes automated document checks, biometrics and other market-dependent verification methods across a broad country and document set.

**Good fit:** Crypto, fintech, marketplace and global digital businesses that prefer to coordinate onboarding and continuing risk controls within one configurable operational platform.

**Potential limitation:** Test each target country and risk tier. Confirm applicant, document, KYB, AML and transaction-monitoring scope, decision transparency, manual-review process, false-positive governance, data location, biometric treatment and pricing across retries and monitoring.

### Trulioo

Trulioo presents an integrated global identity platform for person and business verification, combining data intelligence, document verification, fraud signals and customizable workflows. Its business verification capabilities cover company data and beneficial-owner or officer checks across many jurisdictions.

**Good fit:** Enterprises that need one contract and integration for KYC and KYB across a wide geographic footprint and value access to local data sources alongside document checks.

**Potential limitation:** Country coverage should be validated at the required assurance level, not as a single global number. Test local match rates, document fallback, business registries, beneficial-owner depth, consent, decision reasons, manual review and the handling of thin-file users.

### Veriff

Veriff presents AI-assisted identity verification centered on government-issued documents, selfie capture, liveness, biometrics, device and network signals, with automated and hybrid human-review options. Its platform also includes AML screening and business verification products.

**Good fit:** Consumer-facing digital platforms that place strong weight on guided capture, conversion, document authenticity, liveness and fraud resistance during remote onboarding.

**Potential limitation:** Test real devices, lighting, languages, demographics and attack patterns from target markets. Review biometric consent, retention, human-review location, webhook security, reason codes, appeal workflow, AML scope and performance commitments in contract.

## What buyers should understand before shortlisting

### Coverage must be measured locally

A provider can support a country while delivering poor results for a particular document, demographic or data source. Build a labeled test set for each launch market and measure completion, pass, fail, review and abandonment. Separate genuine-user friction from fraud detection and compare reason codes.

### KYC, KYB and AML are different controls

Identity verification asks whether evidence supports a claimed identity. Business verification establishes entity and ownership information. AML screening and monitoring evaluate risk signals over time. One interface may orchestrate all three, but policy, evidence and accountability remain distinct.

### Biometric and privacy governance

Document and selfie workflows can involve sensitive biometric and identity data. Map consent, lawful basis, purpose, storage location, retention, deletion, model improvement use, human access and subprocessors. Design an exception and appeal route for users who cannot complete the automated flow.

## Best fit by buyer scenario

| Buyer scenario | Likely starting point | Reason to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Crypto platform combining onboarding and transaction monitoring | Sumsub after end-to-end workflow testing | A unified risk profile may reduce handoffs, but controls and investigator ownership still need explicit governance. |
| Global B2B marketplace onboarding companies and owners | Trulioo after country-level KYB validation | Business registry access, beneficial ownership and local fallback procedures are central to the decision. |
| Consumer app facing document and deepfake fraud | Veriff after attack and conversion testing | Guided capture and liveness may be central, but performance must be measured across the actual user base. |
| Regulated product serving both people and businesses | Run matched KYC and KYB test sets across shortlisted platforms | Person and business verification have different evidence, consent, review and refresh requirements. |
| Expansion into a new country | Revalidate workflow rather than reusing a global configuration | Documents, databases, language, consent, risk and regulatory expectations change by market. |

## Try the applicants who cause support tickets

Use consented test identities from the markets you serve. Include a legitimate applicant whose document scan fails, someone who changes address and a case requiring manual review. Ask which checks ran, why the result was returned, who can overturn it and what evidence you can retain. A headline country count will not tell you how the workflow treats your customers.

## Conclusion

Sumsub is a natural starting point for teams seeking a full-cycle platform spanning user and business verification, transaction monitoring, fraud prevention and case management; Trulioo for global person and business verification using a broad data-source network and modular workflows; and Veriff for teams prioritizing document and biometric identity verification, liveness, fraud detection and a guided end-user capture experience. Selection should be based on country-level performance, fraud mix, data policy, manual-review operations and the exact compliance workflow, not global coverage claims alone.
