## Compare the three models

| Model | Investor starts with | Evidence to request | Do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product-led subscription | An issuer or servicing portal | Named applicant-facing entity, subscription agreement and eligibility terms | The portal operator is the appointed distributor |
| Intermediary distribution | A broker, adviser or placement intermediary | Product-specific appointment, eligible investor segments and order-routing agreement | Every investor or geography is permitted |
| Embedded platform access | An app or API presenting the fund | Product integration, contracting entity, order hand-off and statement sample | The integration is itself a distribution appointment |

These models can overlap. Classify the actual subscription path, not the brand or front end.

## What public documents establish

| Product | Documented route or actor | Investor access | What remains open |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FOBXX | [Franklin prospectus](https://www.franklintempleton.com/forms-literature/download-preview/9001-P) describes an app and institutional portal and identifies the transfer-agent record | Follow the prospectus and applicable account terms | Do not infer a universal third-party sales mandate from portal access |
| BUIDL | [Securitize's BUIDL/Tempo disclosure](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Tempo-expands-onchain-yield-offering-with-BlackRocks-BUIDL-fund/default.aspx) names Securitize Markets, LLC as compensated placement agent | Privately offered to investors meeting the fund's applicable criteria | An integration announcement does not itself extend offering eligibility |
| WisdomTree Digital Funds | [WisdomTree Prime's March 31, 2026 disclosure](https://dataspanapi.wisdomtree.com/pdr/documents/REGULATORY_MISC/ETP/US/EN-US/LEGAL-ENTITIES/) names WisdomTree Securities, Inc. as distributor and separately identifies WisdomTree Transfers, Inc. | Check the selected fund prospectus and the applicable app or account terms | The group-level disclosure does not establish all fund-by-fund routes or availability outside its stated scope |
| USTB | [Superstate product disclosure](https://superstate.com/assets/ustb) describes USD or USDC subscriptions and Superstate Services LLC as transfer agent | The product page identifies Accredited Investors and Qualified Purchasers | Independent distribution appointment not established by this source |
| USCC | [Superstate product disclosure](https://superstate.com/assets/uscc) describes account approval and wallet allowlisting | Qualified Purchasers in supported jurisdictions | A DeFi integration is not evidence of an appointed distributor |
| VBDC | [Superstate product disclosure](https://superstate.com/assets/vbdc) describes an investment agreement and USD or USDC funding | Product summary and offering materials define eligibility; review the controlling PPM | External distributor and specific intermediary mandate not established by this source |

These are selected examples, not a distribution census. The named BUIDL appointment is product-specific; the WisdomTree disclosure applies to its Digital Funds as a category and is not a product count. [Fund Comparison](/network-explorer/fund-comparison) tracks four other appointment categories; do not count its named transfer agents as named distributors.

## Follow a subscription from introduction to records

1. Ask who markets the product and which entity signs the investor agreement.
2. Have the parties demonstrate a rejected application and show whose decision and notification are recorded.
3. Trace received cash, the valuation point, confirmed units and the investor statement using one transaction reference.
4. Request a redemption example and verify who handles cutoffs, blocked payments and corrections.
5. Document distribution fees and rights to retain or export investor records if a partner changes.

For an embedded partner, include a broken hand-off in the demonstration: a funded order that has not appeared in the share register must have a named owner and escalation deadline. A working API response is not proof that shares were issued.
