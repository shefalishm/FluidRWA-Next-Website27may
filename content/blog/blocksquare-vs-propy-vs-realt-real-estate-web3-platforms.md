## Vendor profiles

### Blocksquare

Blocksquare describes infrastructure for businesses that want to tokenize real-estate assets and launch investment marketplaces. Its public materials describe a tokenization protocol, a white-label platform and a DeFi-related bridge. Its protocol documentation describes property-linked token economics and a corporate-resolution approach to linking defined revenue rights to a token.

**Good fit:** Owners, operators and market builders that need a technology layer for issuance and distribution rather than a consumer property marketplace.

**Potential limitation:** A technology protocol cannot determine whether an offering, transfer restriction, investor category, marketing practice or property structure is lawful in the intended jurisdiction. Put local securities, property, tax and distribution counsel at the centre of the design.

### Propy

Propy positions itself as a real-estate offer-management and transaction platform for buyers, sellers, agents, title-company representatives and other closing participants. It describes a digital workflow for document management, offers and transaction coordination, with blockchain used as part of its record and automation approach.

**Good fit:** Brokerages, title-adjacent teams and property-sale participants seeking to improve the primary transaction process.

**Potential limitation:** A transaction platform and a tokenization platform solve different problems. Verify local title, escrow, recording, e-signature, identity and document-retention requirements before assuming a workflow can be used for a specific transaction.

### RealT

RealT describes a marketplace through which eligible participants can acquire fractionalized property-linked tokens. Its materials explain that the tokens are associated with a property-specific ownership structure, rather than representing a universal, direct deed model.

**Good fit:** Investors evaluating access to fractionalized real-estate exposure, subject to the platform's eligibility, offering and jurisdictional terms.

**Potential limitation:** Marketplace availability, investor eligibility, property geography, transfer restrictions and liquidity vary. A buyer should review the legal entity, operating agreement, property economics, manager duties, risks and tax consequences for every individual offering.

## Best fit by buyer scenario

| Buyer scenario | Likely starting point | Reason to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Property owner launching a tokenized investment portal | Blocksquare | Its published tooling is directed at asset digitization and white-label marketplace operations. |
| Brokerage digitizing offers and closing | Propy | The central need is transaction coordination rather than fractional issuance. |
| Investor seeking fractional property exposure | RealT | It is positioned as an investor-facing marketplace, subject to eligibility and offering terms. |
| Developer selling a building's units | Propy plus specialist legal and title partners | Digital sale operations do not remove the need for enforceable conveyancing and consumer-protection workflows. |
| Cross-border tokenized property fund | Specialist legal and fund stack first | The product needs securities, fund, tax, custody and distribution design before selecting a software layer. |

## Follow one property through the paperwork

Use a real proposed asset, with permission to share its documents. Ask how title is held, what a buyer actually receives, who collects rent or other income, and what happens on a resale or dispute. Request the legal documents alongside the product screens. The token display is useful only when the property rights and ongoing servicing behind it are clear.

## Conclusion

Choose Blocksquare when you are an owner, operator or platform building tokenized-real-estate issuance and distribution infrastructure; Propy when the core problem is digitizing a property sale and closing workflow; and RealT when the goal is to access a curated marketplace of fractionalized property investments rather than launch an issuer platform.
