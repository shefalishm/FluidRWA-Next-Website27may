For a property sponsor, the most useful comparison starts with the work the sponsor will still have to do after closing. DigiShares describes a white-label real estate offering workflow. Blocksquare documents a property-specific tokenization protocol. Tokeny documents investor onboarding and qualification workflows. Ask each to demonstrate the same offering before comparing proposals.

## Comparison at a glance

| Platform | What its documentation describes | What to ask in the demo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DigiShares | White-label real estate tokenization and investor administration | Show how a failed subscription is reversed and which party reconciles the funds. |
| Blocksquare | A property tokenization protocol with a corporate-resolution workflow | Show the actual agreement for the project and identify who enforces the rights it describes. |
| Tokeny | Investor onboarding and qualification workflows | Show who approves investor eligibility and how a change in status affects a later transfer. |

## Give all three the same property

Send each provider the same one-page brief: a property-owning vehicle, a defined investor population, one subscription currency and a quarterly distribution. Ask them to draw the workflow from signed subscription to the first distribution. Put a named owner beside every approval, record and payment. If a task belongs to the sponsor, a bank or another service provider, put it in the proposal as a dependency.

## Test what happens after closing

Use a failed payment, an ineligible transfer and a distribution returned by the bank. For each scenario, ask the operator to show the exception queue and the evidence the finance team receives. Then request an export of the holder register, transaction history and document versions. Ask what you can take with you if you replace the software. Software demonstrations do not establish that your offering structure is permitted in a particular jurisdiction.

## When this comparison is useful

Use it if you are sponsoring an offering and choosing the software and service providers. For a broader infrastructure shortlist, see [how to compare asset tokenization platforms](/blog/asset-tokenization-platform-comparison).

## Assign each responsibility before the demo

Ask every provider to complete the same responsibility map: who approves subscriptions, who decides eligibility, where the official ownership record lives and who services distributions. For each answer, record the legal entity, the software used and the evidence the issuer can export. Leave a row unresolved if the answer depends on a partner that has not been named in the proposal.
