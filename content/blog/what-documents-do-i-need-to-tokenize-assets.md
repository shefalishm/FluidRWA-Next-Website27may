## Build the document checklist around the transaction

Start by writing down what an investor will legally hold. A token could record a fund share, a claim against an issuer or another interest. The documentation for those arrangements is not interchangeable. Ask qualified counsel to map the asset, issuer, offering and investor jurisdictions before selecting a template or circulating anything to investors.

| Stage | Documents or records to request | Who should explain them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish the asset and issuer | Evidence of title or the underlying claim, governing documents, entity approvals and relevant restrictions | Asset owner and counsel |
| Set the offering terms | Instrument terms, applicable disclosures, subscription agreement and investor eligibility process | Issuer and counsel |
| Prepare operations | Administration, platform and custody agreements as applicable; transfer rules; data retention and exit terms | Issuer, administrator and service providers |
| Accept and record a subscription | Signed agreement, eligibility decision, payment evidence and authoritative ownership entry | Onboarding operations and recordkeeper |
| Service the asset | Distribution or redemption instructions, register updates, investor notices and exception approvals | Administrator or servicing team |

## Trace one subscription before approving the documents

Suppose an investor is approved for 100 fund units but pays for only 90. Decide whether the transaction is rejected, amended or partially accepted under the actual documents. Find who is authorized to make the decision, where it is recorded and what the investor sees. Then match the approval, funds received and number of units recorded. If one system reports 100 and another reports 90, someone needs clear authority to stop issuance and resolve the difference.

Run the same exercise for a transfer to an ineligible investor. The offering documents, the platform's transfer rules and the recordkeeper's procedure should lead to the same result. A configuration screen cannot authorize a transfer the instrument terms prohibit.

## What to ask the platform for

Request a sample export for one issuance and one transfer. Check that records carry a document version, an investor reference, dates and the person or system that approved the action. Ask how the issuer retrieves those records if it changes administrator or platform. Avoid collecting investor identity documents in a shared procurement folder; demonstrate the workflow with synthetic records.

Use this as a preparation checklist, not a list of documents required in every jurisdiction. For procurement, start with the [tokenization platform directory](/vendors/tokenization-platforms) and give each shortlisted provider the same workflow to demonstrate.
