## The short answer

AU10TIX, IDnow and iDenfy all support digital identity verification, but their product emphasis differs. AU10TIX highlights automated document verification, biometrics, NFC and fraud controls. IDnow positions a broader European trust platform spanning identity checks, eIDs and related regulated workflows. iDenfy brings identity verification together with KYB, AML and proof-of-address modules.

No provider should be selected from a generic accuracy claim. Performance varies by country, document, device, lighting, applicant population and attack pattern. The only useful comparison is a controlled pilot with ground truth and a documented manual-review path.

## Side-by-side comparison

| Decision factor | AU10TIX | IDnow | iDenfy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary orientation | Automated identity and document verification with fraud controls | European identity verification and digital trust workflows | Modular identity, KYB, AML and verification platform |
| Strong starting point | High-volume document and biometric onboarding | Regulated European onboarding, eID, video or signature requirements | Teams wanting several onboarding checks from one provider |
| Evidence to request | Results by country and document, fraud testing and review process | Method availability by market, trust-service boundaries and fallback flows | Module ownership, third-party sources, review service and API behavior |
| Main diligence risk | Relying on an aggregate automation or fraud claim | Assuming every verification method is available in every jurisdiction | Treating a broad module list as equal maturity across all checks |

## AU10TIX

AU10TIX publishes identity document verification capabilities that include document capture, authenticity checks, biometrics and NFC-supported flows. It can be relevant where document automation and identity-fraud detection are central to onboarding.

**Good fit:** Digital platforms processing varied identity documents at scale and prepared to measure fraud and conversion outcomes by cohort.

**Verify:** Country and document coverage, liveness method, device requirements, manual review, fraud consortium or third-party dependencies, explainability, data residency, retention, deletion and customer appeal.

## IDnow

IDnow presents the IDnow Trust Platform across identity verification and adjacent digital-trust services. Its European orientation can matter for teams evaluating eID, video identification, document verification or electronic-signature journeys.

**Good fit:** European regulated products that need to combine an identity method with market-specific trust or onboarding requirements.

**Verify:** Method availability by country, qualified trust-service roles, subcontractors, reviewer location and training, fallback journeys, accessibility, data storage and the contractual responsibility for each service.

## iDenfy

iDenfy offers KYC software alongside KYB, AML, face authentication and proof-of-address capabilities. This breadth can reduce the number of interfaces a smaller compliance team needs to manage.

**Good fit:** Fintech, Web3 and digital-service teams that want modular identity and business-verification workflows from a common provider.

**Verify:** Which checks are proprietary or third-party, source coverage, review operations, sanctions and PEP data, case-management integration, webhook reliability, rate limits, data deletion and support.

## How to run a fair KYC pilot

Define the decision before choosing the technology. Document who must be verified, the assurance required, acceptable evidence, mandatory screening and when a human reviewer can override an automated result.

Build a consented test set that reflects real applicants. Include supported documents, damaged documents, transliteration, changed names, low-end devices, poor connectivity and legitimate users who are difficult to verify. Add controlled presentation attacks and manipulated documents with appropriate legal and security controls.

Measure false acceptance, false rejection, abandonment, completion time, manual-review rate and appeal outcomes. Segment every result by country, document and device. A single global pass rate can conceal where the system fails.

## Avoiding cannibalisation

FluidRWA already compares Sumsub, Persona, Trulioo, Veriff, Jumio, Alloy, Socure and blockchain analytics providers in separate guides. This article has a narrower identity-verification scope and a new vendor trio. It should support, not compete with, the broader KYC directory and category guides.

## Buyer checklist

1. Define the assurance level and legal basis.
2. List countries, documents, entities and user cohorts.
3. Test conversion and fraud outcomes with ground truth.
4. Map automated, manual and appeal decisions.
5. Review biometric, residency, retention and deletion terms.
6. Identify every third-party data and review dependency.
7. Preserve an exit path for evidence and audit records.
