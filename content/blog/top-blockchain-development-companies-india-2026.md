## Where might each firm fit?

### Minddeft: broad application and contract scope

[Minddeft](https://www.minddeft.com/) lists an Ahmedabad office and describes smart-contract, enterprise blockchain and tokenization services. A broad menu is a reason to request a narrower, project-specific proposal: which chain, which contract modules, which back-office integrations, and which deliverables are covered? Ask to meet the proposed lead engineer rather than treating a services list as evidence of deployment experience.

### Maticz: application development and platform questions

[Maticz](https://maticz.com/blockchain-development-company) lists a Madurai address and advertises blockchain development. When evaluating an offer, ask whether the proposed product is bespoke engineering, a configurable codebase or a licensed platform. That distinction affects IP ownership, audit scope, upgrades and exit options. Obtain a system diagram and repository-access terms before comparing price.

### Antier: infrastructure and tokenization breadth

[Antier](https://www.antier.com/) publishes Mohali and Bengaluru offices and describes tailored blockchain applications and tokenization infrastructure. A complex RWA project needs more than issuance: seek concrete answers on custody, transfer restrictions, asset servicing, reconciliation and incident ownership. Treat published client and performance statements as vendor claims until separately substantiated.

## An India-specific RFP that produces comparable answers

1. State the business workflow and the smallest production release. Avoid asking every firm for an undefined “blockchain platform.”
2. Require the named delivery location and time-zone overlap for architects, developers, QA and support.
3. Ask for source-code ownership, reusable-component licensing and subcontractor disclosure.
4. Specify independent security review and who pays for remediation or retesting.
5. Break out integration work for identity, custody, payments, accounting and reporting.
6. Request milestone acceptance criteria, documentation and post-launch response commitments.

For an RWA brief, have counsel define the legal and operational requirements before locking the on-chain architecture. Technical vendors should explain how they implement constraints, not promise that code by itself makes a product compliant.
