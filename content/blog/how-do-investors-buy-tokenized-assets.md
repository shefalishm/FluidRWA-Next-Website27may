## What to check

Before sending funds, verify the issuer, the approved payment instructions and whether the purchase is a primary subscription or a secondary trade.

## Work through an example

Trace a purchase from the signed agreement to the final statement. Ask what happens if funds arrive but allocation fails, and who handles the refund.
