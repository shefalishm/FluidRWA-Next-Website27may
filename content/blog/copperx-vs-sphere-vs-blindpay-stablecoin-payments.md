## Side-by-side comparison

| Decision factor | Copperx | Sphere | BlindPay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary orientation | Stablecoin payments for internet businesses | Developer infrastructure for compliant money movement | Stablecoin API for global pay-ins and payouts |
| Typical workflow | Checkout, payment links, invoices and business payment operations | Embedded cross-border payments and orchestration | Local rails, virtual accounts and beneficiary payouts |
| Buyer focus | Merchant conversion and finance operations | Programmable product integration | Country and bank-rail reach |
| Good fit | Online businesses accepting and managing stablecoin revenue | Fintechs embedding stablecoin money movement | Platforms paying or collecting across multiple markets |
| Main diligence point | Settlement, refunds, tax records and merchant support | Licensing chain, reserves, partners and programme controls | Entity eligibility, beneficiary checks, rail availability and returns |
| Do not assume | Every stablecoin or country is supported | One integration removes regulatory responsibility | A quoted payout speed applies to every corridor |

## Vendor profiles

### Copperx

Copperx positions its product around stablecoin payments for internet businesses, including checkout, invoices, payment links and operational tools.

**Good fit:** Merchants and software businesses that want a recognizable payment workflow rather than assembling raw wallet and banking APIs.

**Potential limitation:** Confirm customer eligibility, supported networks, settlement assets, refunds, disputes, reconciliation exports and the contractual provider for each fiat service.

### Sphere

Sphere offers developer-focused infrastructure for compliant global money movement using stablecoins and fiat rails.

**Good fit:** Fintech and platform teams embedding cross-border collections, conversions or payouts into their own product.

**Potential limitation:** Map each corridor to a licensed or regulated entity and partner. Verify reserve, safeguarding, screening, failure handling and pricing rather than relying on a generic coverage statement.

### BlindPay

BlindPay presents APIs for stablecoin-based global pay-ins and payouts, including local payment rails and virtual account-style workflows in supported markets.

**Good fit:** Platforms needing to collect from or pay beneficiaries across markets through a programmable integration.

**Potential limitation:** Country availability changes. Confirm sender and recipient eligibility, purpose restrictions, account ownership, FX, return handling and documentation for each corridor.

## Best fit by buyer scenario

| Buyer scenario | Likely starting point | Reason to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS accepting stablecoins | Copperx | Checkout, invoicing and finance workflows are likely to matter most. |
| Embedded remittance product | Sphere or BlindPay | Corridor reach, compliance allocation, beneficiary experience and returns should drive the test. |
| Marketplace payouts | BlindPay or Sphere | Compare beneficiary onboarding, batch operations, status webhooks, reconciliation and failed-payment recovery. |
| Tokenized asset distributions | Any after legal review | Investor eligibility, withholding, transfer restrictions and records can be more important than payment speed. |
| Global enterprise treasury | Broader bank and treasury review | These tools may handle payment legs but do not automatically replace treasury policy, custody, FX and accounting systems. |

## Pilot the exception, not just the payout

Choose one corridor and recipient type. Send a test payout, then repeat it with an incorrect bank detail, a delayed stablecoin transfer and a recipient who fails a required check. Record the status each party can see and who owns the funds at every step. Ask for the export that lets finance reconcile the instruction, blockchain transaction, conversion and final receipt.

## Conclusion

Choose Copperx when merchant checkout, invoicing and payment operations are the central workflow; Sphere when a developer platform for compliant cross-border money movement and stablecoin orchestration fits the product; and BlindPay when global pay-ins, payouts and local or virtual account rails are the primary need.
