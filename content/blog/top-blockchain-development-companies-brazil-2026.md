## What do the public descriptions actually suggest?

### Cheesecake Labs: product-engineering-led blockchain work

[Cheesecake Labs](https://cheesecakelabs.com/blog/cheesecake-labs-extends-its-blockchain-services/) describes blockchain services that include tokenization and stablecoin-related work; its [company material](https://cheesecakelabs.com/privacy-policy) locates it in Florianopolis. Ask whether the team will deliver the entire user journey, from wallet onboarding through reconciliation, or only a contract/API layer. Request named ownership for security reviews and production support.

### BBChain: enterprise DLT architecture

[BBChain](https://www.bbchain.com.br/pt-br/sobre-nos) publishes a Sao Paulo address and describes blockchain architecture and integration work. For a bank, infrastructure or consortium brief, the deciding question is interoperability with existing identity, custody, reporting and access-control systems. Ask for a data-flow diagram and a precise operating model, including which party runs nodes and resolves incidents.

### WiseBit: custom software discovery

[WiseBit](https://wisebit.com.br/) describes software engineering across blockchain, AI and Web3 from Sao Paulo. Its public material is less detailed about specific enterprise delivery models than the larger service menus above. That is an **unknown to resolve**, not evidence of missing capability. Request relevant work samples, the proposed engineering team and a scoped design workshop before comparing it against a large integrator.

## Five questions for a Brazil-market brief

1. Will discovery, documentation and support be delivered in Portuguese, English or both?
2. Who is responsible for wallet recovery, key custody and transaction approval?
3. Which local payment, identity, accounting and reporting systems must be integrated?
4. How are source code, cloud accounts, admin keys and intellectual property handed over?
5. What independent security and legal reviews are needed for the particular product?

If the project handles regulated assets or payments, get qualified local guidance on the applicable obligations. This article is a development-vendor shortlist, not a legal or licensing assessment.

## Compare the deliverable, not the logo

Send the same one-page brief to every candidate: users, transaction flow, target chains, required integrations, non-functional requirements and launch criteria. Ask each to return architecture assumptions, named roles, acceptance tests and what is explicitly excluded.
