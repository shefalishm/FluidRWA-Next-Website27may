## Side-by-side comparison

| Decision factor | NOWPayments | Coinbase Commerce | BitPay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary orientation | Crypto payment gateway and business ecosystem for acceptance, conversion and payouts | Coinbase-connected merchant checkout and crypto payment acceptance | Merchant crypto-payment processing, invoices and settlement options |
| Best starting point | Business needing broad asset support and combined payment or payout tooling | Merchant assessing a Coinbase ecosystem payment flow | Merchant seeking an established crypto-payment service with commercial settlement considerations |
| Operational question | How are accepted assets, conversion, balances, payouts and support handled? | Who controls settlement wallets, transaction handling and merchant operations? | How do checkout, settlement, pricing, refunds and reconciliation fit the business? |
| Customer-experience question | Supported wallets, assets, network selection and payment confirmation | Wallet flow, confirmation, refund process and shopper support | Checkout option, payment instructions, customer communication and refund handling |
| Main diligence point | Exact supported assets, service fees, countries and payout availability | Merchant eligibility, service scope, wallet responsibility and current product availability | Supported jurisdictions, settlement options, pricing, dispute process and integration fit |
| Do not assume | Accepting many assets means every asset is suitable for treasury or accounting | A crypto payment is automatically reversible, protected or regulated like a card payment | A gateway solves tax, licensing, sanctions, consumer disclosure or refund obligations |

## Vendor profiles

### NOWPayments

NOWPayments publicly presents a crypto business ecosystem for accepting and managing digital-asset payments, with payment APIs, invoices, payment links, plugins, conversion and mass-payout capabilities. It may be relevant where asset breadth, stablecoin support and operational tooling are important to the merchant.

**Good fit:** Global digital businesses that need to compare acceptance, conversion and payout options across a wide range of digital assets and networks.

**Potential limitation:** Verify the exact merchant entity, assets, countries, settlement routes, fees, conversion timing, custody or balance model, refund workflow and accounting exports that apply to the buyer's business.

### Coinbase Commerce

Coinbase Commerce is publicly presented as a merchant solution for accepting crypto payments within the Coinbase ecosystem. It may be relevant for merchants evaluating a payment flow connected to Coinbase products and a crypto-native settlement model.

**Good fit:** Businesses that already use or wish to evaluate the Coinbase ecosystem and can support the operational responsibility that comes with crypto settlement.

**Potential limitation:** Confirm the currently available product, merchant eligibility, jurisdiction, supported assets and networks, wallet responsibility, refund process and tax or accounting workflow before integrating it into checkout.

### BitPay

BitPay publicly presents merchant payment-processing services for accepting cryptocurrency, with checkout, invoice and settlement-related tools. It may be relevant for businesses seeking a specialist payment provider rather than building a direct wallet-payment flow.

**Good fit:** Merchants that need to compare an established crypto-payment operating model with their settlement, customer-service and reporting requirements.

**Potential limitation:** Payment products differ by merchant location, asset, settlement currency and customer route. Confirm contract terms, pricing, refunds, risk controls, dispute process and finance-team reconciliation evidence.

## Best fit by buyer scenario

| Buyer scenario | Likely starting point | Reason to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Global digital service accepting multiple crypto and stablecoin assets | NOWPayments after settlement and payout verification | The buyer may value broad acceptance, but should standardise which assets it will actually retain or convert. |
| Merchant already operating in the Coinbase ecosystem | Coinbase Commerce after merchant-flow validation | The relevant question is whether the current product and settlement model fit the merchant's entity and operational controls. |
| Merchant comparing managed crypto-payment processing | BitPay after commercial and support review | A managed payment model may fit where reporting, settlement and merchant support are more important than direct wallet control. |
| High-volume stablecoin collections | Treasury and compliance design first | Define wallet governance, conversion, liquidity, sanctions screening, reconciliation and accounting before choosing the checkout interface. |
| Consumer checkout with refunds | Test full payment and refund journeys | A successful payment flow is incomplete if the customer, finance and support teams cannot reliably handle reversals, overpayments or wrong-network transfers. |

## Test payment, refund and reconciliation together

Run a small purchase with the currency and checkout flow you plan to offer. Then test an underpayment, a late payment and a customer asking for a refund after settlement. Ask what the merchant sees in each case, who contacts the customer and which file or API record finance can match to the order. A successful checkout screen alone does not close the payment.

## Conclusion

Choose NOWPayments when a merchant needs to investigate broad cryptocurrency and stablecoin acceptance together with payment tools, conversion and payout workflows; Coinbase Commerce when a business is assessing a Coinbase-connected merchant-payment flow and self-managed crypto settlement approach; and BitPay when a merchant needs to evaluate an established crypto-payment provider with merchant tools and settlement options. Payment acceptance is only one part of the decision: evaluate legal entity, geography, money movement, refund policy, accounting, customer disclosure and operational support before selecting a gateway.
