## Buy the operating brief before buying reach

A wallet team preparing a developer launch needs working integrations, useful documentation and developers who return after the announcement. An agency can deliver a widely viewed campaign while that launch still misses its purpose. Start with the action the audience should take and the evidence your team can retain after the engagement ends.

Coinbound, Lunar Strategy and Serotonin already appear in FluidRWA's growth-marketing catalog. This comparison concerns a multi-channel engagement rather than the narrower press-distribution decision covered in our [crypto PR comparison](/blog/chainwire-vs-marketacross-vs-wachsman-crypto-pr). Official material was reviewed November 6, 2026. No agency was hired, no audience-quality audit was performed and no common-scope quote was obtained.

| Buying question | Coinbound | Lunar Strategy | Serotonin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Documented entry point | Web3 marketing across creators, PR, social and other channels | Ecosystem growth, go-to-market strategy and campaign services | Strategy spanning product, marketing and brand positioning |
| Useful first brief | Creator program tied to a defined launch and conversion event | Ecosystem audience map and coordinated launch plan | Positioning decision and the execution brief it should produce |
| Evidence to request | Creator selection rationale, approved posts and channel-level records | Dependencies, channel owners and weekly decisions | Research inputs, positioning alternatives and accountable deliverables |
| Commercial boundary | Separate agency work from creator and media spend | Confirm payment, cancellation and change scope | Confirm where strategy ends and execution begins |
| Not established here | Comparable acquisition cost or independently verified reach | Incremental adoption attributable to the agency | Comparative conversion improvement or implementation capacity |

These are evaluation starting points, not exclusive service boundaries or a ranking. [Coinbound's service description](https://coinbound.io/web3-marketing-agency/), [Lunar Strategy's services](https://www.lunarstrategy.com/) and [Serotonin's strategy description](https://serotonin.co/strategy) support the limited scope summaries. Serotonin's strategy text was available through official indexed content; the direct page supplied no readable body to the research tool. Request a current written scope before relying on that description.

## Coinbound: inspect the creator workflow behind the coverage report

Coinbound describes influencer and KOL campaigns alongside PR, community, social, paid media and search services. Its [Sui case study](https://coinbound.io/case-studies/sui/) describes creator sourcing, copy, scheduling and weekly reporting. That is agency-reported operating evidence. Its view totals are not an independently audited acquisition result, and they should not become your forecast.

For the illustrative wallet launch, ask for a small proposed creator roster before approving the whole program. Each entry should explain the relevant audience, content format, commercial relationship and intended action. A large following in speculative trading may be a poor match for a developer integration. Require a review of actual recent content rather than a spreadsheet of follower totals.

Define the publication hold point. Who verifies a custody statement, signs off on security language and approves a demonstration transaction? A creator's draft must not imply that a wallet is insured, approved or risk-free because a campaign manager used those words in a briefing document. Keep corrections and final approvals attached to the exact post version.

Ask to see a sample reporting record with the post URL, publication time, paid status, platform-reported metrics and the buyer's own conversion event. If attribution is unavailable, label it unavailable. Do not let estimated impressions silently become verified users or signed commercial customers.

## Lunar Strategy: make the ecosystem plan executable

Lunar's website lists go-to-market strategy, social marketing, PR, events and influencer strategy. It describes strategic planning around product, brand and token context. For an ecosystem launch, the useful deliverable is a sequence of decisions: which audience is being served, which application is ready, what partners must supply and which message may be published when.

Give the team one difficult constraint. For example, the SDK is ready for a test integration, while a proposed partner announcement still lacks permission. Ask for a plan that can proceed without that partner logo. This exposes whether the engagement is built around actual readiness or a calendar of announcements that assumes every dependency will resolve.

Review the [payment terms page](https://www.lunarstrategy.com/terms-of-payment) alongside the proposed agreement. The page is marked January 2, 2025. Ask for written payment timing, cancellation provisions and milestone acceptance for the engagement being quoted, then have the appropriate internal reviewer reconcile them with the signed scope.

Keep channel access in the buyer's control where feasible. Specify who owns advertising accounts, pixels, landing pages and creator contracts. A marketing dashboard is less useful if your team cannot retrieve the source records after a supplier change. An exit rehearsal should be part of the brief, even for a short campaign.

## Serotonin: ask what decision the strategy will settle

Serotonin's official strategy description connects product, marketing and brand positioning. Treat that as a reason to ask for the strategic question, not as proof of a complete outsourced growth department. Confirm the current offering directly, including named staff and execution responsibilities, because the readable source available for this review is limited.

For the wallet example, the question might be whether the product is positioned for application developers or enterprise treasury teams. Those buyers require different evidence, procurement paths and content. A useful strategy deliverable should make the trade-off explicit and connect it to a research record. A polished phrase alone does not settle it.

Request a sample decision document with confidential material removed. Look for rejected alternatives, assumptions and a handoff to execution. Ask who will turn the positioning into a landing page, technical article, sales brief and creator instructions, and which of those outputs are included. The buyer should not discover after approval that another supplier must translate every recommendation.

Strategy acceptance should depend on whether the team can make and explain the decision. It should not depend on an unmeasured promise of future market leadership. Agree how the work changes if interviews contradict the initial hypothesis or a product limitation makes the preferred message inaccurate.

## Use one launch exercise to compare proposals

Send the same short product description, target audience and approval constraints to each shortlisted team. Ask them to mark what they know, what they need from you and what they would refuse to promise. This is a proposed procurement exercise, not a completed vendor test.

| Exercise | Evidence to retain | Decision it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Security claim challenged before publication | Corrected copy and named approver | Whether facts survive campaign pressure |
| Partner announcement delayed | Revised sequence and cost consequence | Whether launch dependencies are understood |
| Creator audience poorly matched | Replacement rationale and approval trail | Whether selection is more than follower count |
| Reporting platform unavailable | Exported source records and explicit gaps | Whether reporting remains inspectable |
| Agency relationship ends | Account inventory, files and access handoff | Whether the buyer can continue operating |

Quote the same work: strategy, staff time, creative production, creator compensation, media spend, monitoring and reporting. Identify pass-through costs and any markup. Define the event that releases the next budget tranche. This article supplies no price estimate because a retainer without a common statement of work is not comparable.

The final shortlist should explain why an agency fits the audience and operating model. Review the [growth-marketing directory](/vendors/growth-marketing), then prepare a brief that your product, marketing and risk owners can all sign off on. Agency selection becomes easier once the work and its acceptance evidence are specific.
