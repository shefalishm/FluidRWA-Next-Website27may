## What to check

Inventory who owns the asset, who can transfer it and who maintains the authoritative record. Identify a workflow that is expensive today.

## Work through an example

Pilot one asset with a defined success measure, such as fewer manual register corrections. Stop if a conventional database solves the same problem more simply.
