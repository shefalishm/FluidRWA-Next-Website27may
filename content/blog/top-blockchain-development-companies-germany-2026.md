## How do the three offerings differ?

### Datarella: DLT architecture with distributed delivery

[Datarella](https://datarella.com/team/) identifies Munich as its headquarters and says its Munich team handles blockchain architecture and DLT system design, with additional development personnel elsewhere. That distinction matters for buyers who require specific access or data-location controls. Ask for an explicit staffing map and confirm which components, environments and credentials each team can access.

### web3.berlin: development plus operations

[web3.berlin](https://web3.berlin/) describes Berlin-based Web3 consulting, development and operations. For a live protocol or application, ask for a runbook covering nodes, indexing, deployments, alerting, upgrades and incident escalation. A builder that also operates infrastructure may simplify accountability, but the contract should still separate build milestones from ongoing service obligations.

### Pixely: blockchain applications and smart contracts

[Pixely](https://pixely.group/blockchain-application-development/) describes blockchain application and smart-contract development, and its [contact page](https://pixely.group/contact/) lists Munich. Ask for an architecture workshop that distinguishes on-chain logic from conventional software, plus the testing and independent review path. Confirm any reuse or licensing terms for pre-existing code.

## Procurement questions that expose real differences

1. Where are the engineers, operators and subcontractors actually located, and who is the contracting entity?
2. Which data must remain off-chain, and who controls access, retention and deletion?
3. Who owns keys, upgrade roles, source repositories, hosting accounts and deployment pipelines?
4. Which security work is done by the developer, and which will be independently reviewed?
5. What evidence will demonstrate that integrations, recovery and incident procedures work?
6. What support is included after launch, and how will a later vendor transition work?

For financial or tokenized-asset products, obtain qualified advice for the applicable legal and regulatory obligations. Technical implementation and regulatory status are separate questions. ## What is still unknown from public pages?

The sources do not give comparable fixed prices, team availability, audit outcomes or production service levels. Keep those fields blank in an internal scorecard until each firm provides documentary evidence. “Not verified” is not the same as “not offered.”
