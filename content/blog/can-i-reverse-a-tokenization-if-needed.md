## What to check

Map who may freeze, cancel, reissue or correct the register. Check what the contract supports and what the offering documents authorize.

## Work through an example

Rehearse a mistaken allocation. Trace the old and replacement balances and confirm the cash leg and ownership register are corrected as well.
