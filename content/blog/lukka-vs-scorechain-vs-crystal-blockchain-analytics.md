Blockchain analytics is no longer only for exchanges and law enforcement. Stablecoin issuers, tokenization platforms, custody providers, payment companies, OTC desks, funds and even enterprise treasury teams need to understand wallet exposure, transaction risk, sanctions risk and counterparty behavior.

Lukka, Scorechain and Crystal Intelligence all operate in blockchain analytics and crypto compliance, but they differ in positioning, buyer fit and workflow depth.

## Short Answer

Choose Lukka if your organization needs institutional-quality blockchain data, transaction and wallet monitoring, analytics and broader enterprise data infrastructure.

Choose Scorechain if you want an AML-focused platform with wallet screening, transaction monitoring, investigations, VASP due diligence and strong European compliance orientation.

Choose Crystal Intelligence if you need compliance monitoring, investigation tooling, case management, visualization and broad blockchain intelligence for regulated digital asset operations.

| Wallet screening | Strong | Strong | Strong |

## What Lukka Is Good For

Lukka's blockchain analytics offering builds on institutional digital asset data, wallet and transaction monitoring, clustering and risk assessment. Lukka also announced its acquisition of Coinfirm in 2024, which matters because many buyers still search for Coinfirm when looking for AML risk reports or blockchain analytics.

Lukka is especially relevant for:

- Institutions with digital asset data programs
- Asset managers and funds
- Exchanges and trading businesses
- Enterprises that need on-chain data connected to reporting workflows
- Compliance teams that need wallet and cluster monitoring
- Organizations that already evaluate Lukka for tax, data or reporting infrastructure

The biggest Lukka strength is that blockchain analytics can sit inside a broader data and reporting environment. For institutions, AML analytics is rarely isolated. It needs to connect to accounting, controls, legal entity structures, regulatory reporting and internal systems.

## Where Lukka May Not Be Ideal

Lukka may be less ideal for a smaller team that wants a lightweight wallet checker, a simple self-serve transaction monitor or a narrowly scoped compliance dashboard. It is more compelling when the buyer wants institutional-grade data coverage and is willing to go through an enterprise buying process.

Ask Lukka:

- Which blockchain protocols are covered today?
- How does entity clustering work?
- What is available through API versus dashboard?
- How are risk scores explained?
- What reporting is available for compliance teams?
- How does the product connect with other Lukka modules?

## What Scorechain Is Good For

Scorechain is positioned as a blockchain analytics and crypto AML compliance platform. It emphasizes wallet screening, transaction monitoring, investigation tools, KYT, VASP due diligence, configurable alerts and European compliance orientation. Its public materials also emphasize EU-hosted infrastructure, MiCA readiness and compliance workflows for crypto businesses and traditional finance.

Scorechain is especially relevant for:

- Exchanges and wallet providers
- Stablecoin and tokenization businesses
- Banks and fintechs entering digital assets
- Compliance teams that need customizable risk scenarios
- Organizations with European regulatory exposure
- Teams that need VASP due diligence and KYT reporting

Scorechain's value is in practical compliance workflow. It can support screening, monitoring, reports, risk customization and investigation without forcing every team to become a blockchain forensics expert.

## Where Scorechain May Not Be Ideal

Scorechain may not be the best fit if the buyer is looking for a broad enterprise data platform beyond AML and blockchain intelligence. It is focused on compliance and analytics, so accounting, tax and operational reporting may need separate tools.

Ask Scorechain:

- Which chains are supported for your specific assets?
- Can risk scenarios be customized by jurisdiction?
- Are sanctions lists updated in real time?
- How does indirect exposure scoring work?
- Does the API support pre-transaction screening?
- What reports are audit-ready?
- Can alerts route into your case management system?

## What Crystal Intelligence Is Good For

Crystal Intelligence is focused on crypto compliance, investigations and monitoring. Its platform materials mention transaction screening, risk scenarios, daily-updated flagged entity data, real-time alerts, case management, SAR narrative support, visualization and entity due diligence.

Crystal is especially relevant for:

- Compliance departments
- Investigation teams
- Exchanges and custodians
- Fintechs and payment providers
- Regulated digital asset businesses
- Teams needing visualization and case workflow

Crystal's strongest fit is when transaction monitoring needs to turn into operational decisions. A dashboard is not enough if analysts need to escalate cases, draft narratives, review entity exposure and keep records for compliance.

## Where Crystal May Not Be Ideal

Crystal may be more than needed if a team only wants a basic address risk score or occasional wallet screening. Buyers should also confirm current chain coverage, pricing, data refresh frequency and jurisdiction-specific support before committing.

## How to Choose for RWA, Stablecoins and Tokenization

For tokenized assets, analytics requirements depend on legal structure. A tokenized fund may need investor wallet screening and transfer monitoring. A stablecoin issuer may need sanctions controls, issuer-side monitoring and redemption-risk workflows. A payment provider may need pre-transaction screening, post-transaction monitoring and SAR escalation.

Map the workflow before comparing demos:

- Do you need pre-transaction screening or post-transaction monitoring?
- Do you need wallet risk, transaction risk or entity risk?
- Do you need direct exposure only or multi-hop exposure?
- Do you need VASP due diligence?
- Do you need case management?
- Do you need API integration?
- Which chains matter today and next year?
- Does compliance need jurisdiction-specific rules?

## Best Fit by Scenario

Use Lukka when blockchain analytics should connect with institutional data, reporting and enterprise workflows.

Use Scorechain when AML monitoring, wallet screening, KYT and European compliance workflow are the main priority.

Use Crystal when investigations, monitoring, case management and compliance operations need to work together in one environment.
