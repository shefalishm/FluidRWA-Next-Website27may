## The short answer

Apex Group, IQ-EQ and JTC all serve alternative-investment structures and publish digital-asset capabilities, but buyers should not assume an identical operating model. Apex Group offers dedicated digital-asset fund services within a broad global platform. IQ-EQ combines digital-asset expertise with administration, compliance and reporting for fund and asset managers. JTC supports digital-asset businesses, token issuers, protocol foundations and crypto funds through administration, governance and wider fiduciary services.

The right decision starts with the fund's legal structure, domicile, assets and authoritative records. Technology should support that model; it should not blur who calculates NAV, maintains investor records, approves valuations or reconciles custody.

## Apex Group

Apex Group publishes digital-asset fund services and technology propositions for managers operating across alternative investments. Its breadth can help managers that want administration, investor services and adjacent capabilities within a larger provider group.

**Good fit:** Established or scaling managers seeking a global administrator with a dedicated digital-asset proposition.

**Verify:** Contracting and regulated entities, supported domiciles, asset and venue integrations, wallet-level accounting, staking and DeFi treatment, valuation policy support, NAV controls, investor servicing, cybersecurity, service team and exit assistance.

## IQ-EQ

IQ-EQ lists digital assets among its fund and asset-manager expertise and combines fund administration with compliance, asset servicing, CFO support, data and reporting services.

**Good fit:** Alternative managers that need digital assets handled within a wider private-markets administration and compliance model.

**Verify:** Digital-asset experience of the assigned team, accounting and reconciliation methodology, source systems, local entity, regulatory scope, investor onboarding, financial statements, reporting and oversight of subcontractors.

## JTC

JTC's digital-assets materials describe support for digital-asset businesses, token issuers, protocol foundations, crypto funds and blockchain companies, with governance, administration and specialist oversight.

**Good fit:** Managers and Web3 organizations that need fund or entity administration together with governance and fiduciary support.

**Verify:** Which service line and entity will deliver the work, fund-administration depth for the selected domicile, wallet and custody reconciliation, valuation, compliance roles, governance appointments, technology stack and incident responsibilities.

## The operating model to define first

Document the authoritative record for investors, ownership, cash, tokens and portfolio assets. Name the owner of subscriptions, capital activity, transfer restrictions, NAV, fee calculations, valuations, financial statements, regulatory reports and investor communications.

Digital assets add operational questions. The administrator needs complete transaction data from custodians, wallets, exchanges and protocols. Transfers require classification; gas fees, rewards, staking, forks, airdrops and smart-contract positions require accounting policies. Pricing sources and valuation times must be agreed before the first NAV cycle.

Tokenization introduces another record. The legal register, administrator books and token ledger must reconcile through subscriptions, redemptions and transfers. A tokenization platform can automate workflows, but it does not silently become the fund administrator or transfer agent.

## Service Scope Matrix

Use this matrix in the RFP. Require the proposed contracting entity to mark each item as delivered directly, delivered by an affiliate, subcontracted, integrated or excluded.

| Workstream | Evidence to request |
|---|---|
| Fund accounting and NAV | Sample NAV pack, pricing hierarchy, cutoff policy, approval workflow and exception log |
| Digital-asset bookkeeping | Wallet and venue ingestion, transaction classification, fee and reward treatment, unsupported-asset process |
| Investor onboarding | KYC ownership, document workflow, beneficial ownership, eligibility and escalation responsibilities |
| Registry and transfer agency | Authoritative investor record, capital activity, transfer restrictions, corrections and communication duties |
| Financial statements | Accounting framework, digital-asset policy support, audit coordination and reporting timetable |
| Regulatory and tax reporting | Jurisdictions, filings, data owners, review responsibilities and exclusions |
| Governance and entity services | Board, officer, company-secretarial, substance and fiduciary responsibilities |
| Technology and data | Portal, APIs, file interfaces, identifiers, history, access model and export formats |

Do not accept a group-level capability statement when the proposal depends on a different country, affiliate or subcontractor. The service schedule should name the entity, team, system and operating location for every material responsibility.

## Digital-Asset Accounting Questions

Ask each provider to classify the same representative data set. Include custody transfers, exchange trades, gas fees, staking rewards, validator penalties, forks, airdrops, token migrations, wrapped assets and one smart-contract position.

The objective is not to force identical accounting conclusions. It is to see whether the provider has a governed policy, can explain source data and preserves the evidence used in the NAV. Require a clear process for unidentified transactions, missing cost basis, stale prices and assets that the accounting platform cannot classify automatically.

## Implementation and Parallel-Close Test

| Test | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Opening position migration | Cash, investor capital, asset quantities and historical cost reconcile to signed opening balances |
| Wallet and custodian ingestion | Every source is complete, time-aligned and linked to a stable account identifier |
| Pricing exception | A missing or disputed price follows the approved hierarchy and receives documented review |
| Investor capital event | Subscription or redemption connects payment, investor record, units and ledger posting |
| Digital-asset exception | Unknown transfer, reward or fee is held for review without silently distorting NAV |
| Reporting close | Draft NAV, investor statements and reporting pack arrive within the proposed timetable |
| Complete export | Books, investors, transactions, documents, approvals and history can be reconstructed outside the portal |

Run at least two parallel closes. The first identifies data and policy gaps; the second tests whether the provider actually corrected them. Measure unresolved exceptions and manual dependencies, not only whether a final number was produced.

## How to run a selection process

Give each provider the same fund structure, domicile, asset inventory, expected activity, investor profile and reporting calendar. Ask for a responsibility matrix, data-flow diagram, sample reports, implementation plan and named service team.

Run a parallel close using representative wallet and custodian data. Include a failed or unidentified transfer, a staking reward, a valuation exception, a late subscription and an investor-record correction. Measure how quickly the provider identifies and resolves breaks.

Review commercial terms beyond the base fee: onboarding, entities, investors, transactions, special assets, statements, tax support, regulatory reporting, audit support, data interfaces and termination assistance.

## Buyer checklist

1. Define the legal structure and domicile.
2. Identify every authoritative record and accountable owner.
3. Agree valuation and accounting policies for digital assets.
4. Test custody, wallet, exchange and protocol data flows.
5. Reconcile investor records, books and token balances.
6. Interview the named service team.
7. Contract for data portability and transition assistance.
