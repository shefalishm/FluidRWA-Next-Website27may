## The short answer

Ondo OUSG is the natural starting point for qualified institutional investors prioritizing 24/7 stablecoin subscriptions and redemptions. Superstate USTB is designed for qualified purchasers seeking a tokenized private fund with continuous NAV and protocol-level minting and redemption. Franklin Templeton's FOBXX, represented onchain as BENJI tokens, is a U.S. registered government money market fund with blockchain-integrated transfer-agent recordkeeping.

The products should not be compared as three versions of the same stablecoin. They have different legal structures, investor gates, official records, portfolios, operating windows and distribution models. Yield is also variable and date-sensitive, so it should not be the first or only selection criterion.

## Side-by-side comparison

| Decision factor | Ondo OUSG | Superstate USTB | Franklin Templeton FOBXX / BENJI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product form | Interests in a private fund | Tokenized private fund | Registered U.S. government money market fund |
| Intended investor | Accredited investors and qualified purchasers, subject to restrictions | Qualified purchasers in supported jurisdictions | Eligible retail and institutional investors through supported channels |
| Portfolio orientation | Short-term Treasury and money-market exposure, with liquidity assets | Short-duration U.S. Treasury bills | At least 99.5% in government securities, cash and fully collateralized repo under its prospectus |
| Share record | Fund and servicing records connected to tokenized interests | Token or book-entry ownership | Transfer agent's proprietary recordkeeping system integrated with public blockchains |
| Liquidity proposition | 24/7 stablecoin mint and redemption is a core product claim | Real-time accrual and protocol mint/redeem features; market-day liquidity is documented | Purchases and redemptions follow the fund's prospectus and supported account channels |
| Onchain orientation | Institutional cash management and collateral utility | Onchain fund distribution and DeFi integrations | Blockchain-enabled mutual-fund recordkeeping and peer-to-peer transfer subject to rules |
| First diligence question | Who is eligible and what creates reliable 24/7 liquidity? | Which share class, network and redemption process applies? | Which network, account channel and transfer restrictions apply to the investor? |

## Product profiles

### Ondo OUSG

OUSG gives eligible institutions exposure to a short-term U.S. Treasury strategy through interests in an Ondo-managed private fund. Ondo states that the product supports instant, 24/7 subscriptions and redemptions using stablecoins and that its portfolio includes institutional tokenized funds, stablecoins and bank deposits for liquidity.

**Good fit:** Crypto-native institutions that already operate with approved wallets and stablecoins and want treasury exposure that can participate in onchain cash-management or collateral workflows.

**Verify before proceeding:** Fund eligibility, issuing entity, current holdings, liquidity buffer, mint and redemption limits, fees, supported networks, transfer restrictions, administrator records and treatment during a market or stablecoin disruption.

### Superstate USTB

USTB is the share token for the Superstate Short Duration U.S. Government Securities Fund. Superstate describes tokenized or book-entry ownership, real-time interest accrual, protocol mint and redeem functionality, and integrations with selected DeFi protocols. The fund is limited to qualified purchasers in supported jurisdictions.

**Good fit:** Qualified investors and protocols that value a tokenized private-fund structure, continuous NAV mechanics and explicit onchain integration pathways.

**Verify before proceeding:** Current investment manager and sub-adviser, supported chains, wallet approval process, NAV source, market-day versus continuous processing, protocol exposure, redemption liquidity, custody and transfer permissions.

### Franklin Templeton FOBXX / BENJI

The Franklin OnChain U.S. Government Money Fund is a registered government money market fund. One fund share corresponds to one BENJI token, while the transfer agent maintains the official ownership record through a proprietary system integrated with public blockchains. Its regulatory filings make clear that blockchain recordkeeping does not change the underlying investment portfolio or remove investment risk.

**Good fit:** Investors that want a regulated money-market-fund structure and blockchain-enabled recordkeeping without using a private-fund wrapper.

**Verify before proceeding:** Investor and jurisdiction eligibility, supported network, authoritative record, purchase and sale procedures, expense limits, yield calculation, peer-to-peer transfer rules, wallet recovery and the distinction between the fund share and a stablecoin.

## The five decisions that matter most

### 1. Legal claim before token symbol

Ask what the token legally represents and against whom the holder has a claim. A private partnership interest, private statutory-trust share and registered mutual-fund share do not produce the same rights simply because each can be held in a wallet.

### 2. Authoritative record

Determine whether the blockchain is the official ownership record, one component of an integrated transfer-agent system or a distribution representation reconciled to another register. Document what happens when records disagree.

### 3. Liquidity under stress

"24/7" may describe order submission, stablecoin settlement or a maintained liquidity facility rather than guaranteed liquidation of the entire position at any size. Ask about cut-offs, gates, reserves, bank hours, stablecoin dependencies and exceptional-market procedures.

### 4. Eligibility and transfer controls

Map KYC, accreditation or qualified-purchaser checks, wallet allowlisting, geographic exclusions and secondary-transfer rules. An integration is not usable if the receiving wallet or protocol cannot lawfully hold the asset.

### 5. Operational utility

Decide whether the requirement is simply treasury yield, onchain collateral, protocol liquidity, programmable distribution or registered-fund access. Paying for composability has little value when policy requires all assets to remain in a conventional custody account.

## Best fit by buyer scenario

| Buyer scenario | Likely starting point | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Crypto-native institutional treasury | OUSG | Stablecoin-based minting, redemption and collateral utility are central to the proposition. |
| DeFi protocol integrating a permissioned treasury asset | USTB | Protocol mint/redeem mechanics and documented integrations deserve investigation. |
| U.S. investor seeking a registered money market fund | FOBXX / BENJI | The registered fund and transfer-agent model is materially different from private products. |
| Treasury team requiring conventional custody only | Compare book-entry and custodied options across all three | Wallet availability should not override custody policy. |
| Product team building around tokenized collateral | OUSG or USTB, subject to eligibility | Test transfer, liquidation, price and redemption behavior inside the exact workflow. |

## Procurement and integration checklist

1. Obtain the current offering document or prospectus and identify the issuing legal entity.
2. Confirm investor eligibility for every intended entity and beneficial owner.
3. Map subscription, redemption, transfer, income distribution and wallet-recovery workflows.
4. Record the authoritative ownership ledger and reconciliation process.
5. Test accepted payment assets, processing windows, limits, fees and failure handling.
6. Review the underlying portfolio, liquidity buffer, valuation method and service providers.
7. Confirm custody, network and smart-contract dependencies.
8. Model a stablecoin depeg, chain outage, bank closure and large redemption.
9. Verify exportable statements, tax records, audit evidence and transaction identifiers.
10. Obtain legal, tax and investment advice for the intended jurisdiction and holder.

## Final recommendation

Choose the legal and operating model before comparing headline yield. OUSG is strongest as an institutional, crypto-native treasury and collateral proposition. USTB emphasizes tokenized private-fund operations, continuous NAV and protocol connectivity. Franklin Templeton FOBXX offers a registered government money market fund whose shareholder record uses blockchain-integrated infrastructure.
