## What to check

The [EIB issuance announcement](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2021-141-european-investment-bank-eib-issues-its-first-ever-digital-bond-on-a-public-blockchain) documents a completed EUR 100 million transaction. It is evidence of issuance and settlement, not current trading volume.

## Work through an example

The [BlackRock BUIDL case study](/use-cases/blackrock-buidl-tokenized-treasury-fund-case-study) traces a launched fund and its servicing roles. For either example, distinguish an announced pilot, a completed transaction and evidence of repeated use.
