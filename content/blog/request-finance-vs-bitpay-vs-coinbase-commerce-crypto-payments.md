## Crypto Payments Are Not One Category

Businesses often say they need "crypto payments," but that phrase can mean very different things.

One company may need invoices for contractors and vendors. Another may need checkout for customers. A fund may need stablecoin subscriptions and redemptions. A protocol may need treasury payments. An enterprise may need payment links, settlement, accounting exports and finance controls.

Request Finance, BitPay and Coinbase Commerce all operate in crypto payments, but they are not interchangeable.

## Quick Comparison

| Provider | Strongest fit | Less ideal when |
|---|---|---|
| Request Finance | B2B crypto invoices, accounts payable, receivables and finance workflows | The buyer mainly needs simple merchant checkout for retail customers |
| BitPay | Merchant crypto checkout, payment processing and settlement | The buyer needs deep internal invoice approval and accounts payable workflow |
| Coinbase Commerce | Crypto checkout for merchants connected to Coinbase's ecosystem | The buyer needs broader finance operations, multi-entity invoice controls or vendor-payment workflows |

## Short Answer

Request Finance is strongest for finance teams that need structured crypto invoicing and payment operations.

BitPay is strongest for merchants that want to accept crypto and settle in a business-friendly way.

Coinbase Commerce is strongest for businesses that want crypto checkout with Coinbase ecosystem familiarity.

## Request Finance: Best Fit For B2B Crypto Invoices

Request Finance is built around invoices, payments, accounts payable, receivables and finance-team workflows. It is often more relevant to operating teams than to simple consumer checkout.

Request Finance may fit when the buyer needs:

- crypto invoices
- vendor payments
- contractor payments
- accounts payable workflows
- multi-currency payment operations
- stablecoin payments
- finance approvals
- payment records and exports

For Web3 teams, this can be practical because many vendors, contributors, agencies and infrastructure providers already accept stablecoins or crypto. For RWA teams, it may be relevant for operating expenses, service-provider payments or internal treasury operations, although regulated investor flows require additional controls.

Request Finance is less ideal when the main need is a consumer checkout button for a high-volume merchant site.

## BitPay: Best Fit For Merchant Crypto Checkout

BitPay is one of the more established crypto payment processors. It is usually evaluated by merchants that want to accept crypto without building wallet, conversion and settlement infrastructure themselves.

BitPay may fit when the buyer needs:

- merchant checkout
- payment buttons
- invoices or payment links
- settlement support
- volatility management
- business payment processing
- broad customer-facing crypto acceptance

The buyer should review supported countries, settlement options, fees, transaction flow, refund handling and supported assets. For businesses that want customer checkout rather than internal payment operations, BitPay can be easier to frame than finance-workflow tools.

BitPay is less ideal when the buyer needs a full accounts payable workspace or deep Web3-native treasury workflow.

## Coinbase Commerce: Best Fit For Coinbase-Connected Checkout

Coinbase Commerce is designed for businesses that want to accept crypto payments through a Coinbase-supported merchant product. It benefits from Coinbase's brand familiarity and crypto infrastructure.

Coinbase Commerce may fit when the buyer needs:

- crypto checkout
- payment links
- merchant payment acceptance
- integration with Coinbase ecosystem tooling
- familiar crypto brand for customers
- business-facing checkout workflows

For teams already using Coinbase products or serving customers who trust Coinbase, the brand familiarity can reduce friction.

Coinbase Commerce is less ideal when the buyer needs a broad finance operations platform, accounts payable controls or custom stablecoin treasury workflows.

## Buyer Framework

| Buyer need | Likely fit |
|---|---|
| Paying contractors and Web3 vendors | Request Finance |
| Consumer merchant checkout | BitPay or Coinbase Commerce |
| Crypto invoice management | Request Finance |
| Checkout with Coinbase brand familiarity | Coinbase Commerce |
| Settlement-oriented merchant processing | BitPay |
| Stablecoin operating payments | Request Finance |

## What Buyers Should Confirm

- Which countries are supported?
- Which coins and stablecoins are supported?
- Can the business settle in fiat, crypto or stablecoins?
- What are the fees?
- Is checkout hosted, embedded or API-driven?
- Are refunds supported?
- Are accounting exports available?
- How are failed or underpaid transactions handled?
- What compliance checks are built in?
- Can the provider support recurring payments or invoice schedules?

## Final View

Crypto payment provider selection should start with the payment workflow.

If the workflow is B2B finance, invoices and operating payments, Request Finance is usually the more relevant starting point. If the workflow is merchant checkout, BitPay and Coinbase Commerce are more natural comparisons.

For RWA and tokenization teams, payments should also be evaluated alongside KYC, investor eligibility, sanctions screening, custody, banking partners and legal structure. Payment convenience alone is not enough.
