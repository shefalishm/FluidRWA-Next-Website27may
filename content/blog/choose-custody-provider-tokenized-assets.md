## Start with the instrument and the ownership record

A custodian may hold a token or control a wallet without maintaining the legal register for the underlying asset. Ask the issuer, transfer agent and custodian which record controls ownership and how discrepancies are corrected. Identify the legal entity that contracts with you, any subcustodian and what rights clients have to recover assets if a provider fails.

## Ask for evidence, not feature names

| Decision | Evidence to request |
| --- | --- |
| Can the asset be held and moved? | Written support for the exact network, contract and transfer restrictions |
| Who can authorize a transfer? | Role matrix, approval thresholds and the audit history of policy changes |
| How are holdings reconciled? | Sample account statement and a trace from wallet activity to the client entitlement record |
| Can you recover or exit? | Recovery runbook, named contacts, export format and asset migration terms |
| What happens if a provider fails? | Contract terms on segregation, subcustody and treatment of client assets |

## Run one approval and one recovery exercise

In a sandbox, ask one operator to initiate a transfer to an approved address and a second operator to review it. Then change the destination to an unapproved address and verify that it cannot proceed without the required authorization. Capture the resulting record, including who approved or rejected it. Separately, walk through losing access to one approver: who can restore access, how long does it take, and can the emergency process move assets without the normal safeguards?

## Compare the service you are actually buying

MPC describes a signing design, not the legal status of a custody service. For example, [NYDFS custody guidance](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry-guidance/industry-letters/il20250930-updated-guidance-custodial-structures) addresses customer protection for the virtual-currency entities it covers; do not apply it as a global rule. Have counsel confirm the requirements for the product and jurisdiction. Use the [custody directory](/vendors/crypto-custody-providers) to identify candidates, then run the same tests for each.
