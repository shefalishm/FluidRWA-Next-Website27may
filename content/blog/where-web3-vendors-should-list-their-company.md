FluidRWA publishes this guide and sells listing services. This is not an independent ranking of directories. Listing or verification on any channel does not guarantee enquiries, Google positions or AI citations.

## RWA.xyz: use the correct company type

Its documentation separates [platform and issuer onboarding](https://docs.rwa.xyz/onboarding/platforms) from service-provider onboarding. Use the path that reflects what the company actually does. The published process uses a company email in the Partners App, then an existing-company claim or a new-company submission for review.

An issuer listing can also involve asset information and data integration. A consultancy or software provider should not submit a tokenized asset it does not issue merely to obtain a profile. Ask the platform which category fits if the business spans several roles. Review timelines and approval remain the platform's decision.

## The Grid: claim the profile you control

The Grid's published process describes a project identifier, an administrator contact and proof of ownership before editing the profile. It describes claiming as free. That statement is about the claim process, not every possible service, and should be checked against the current terms before engagement.

Use an organization-controlled administrator account rather than a departing employee's personal inbox. Record who can update descriptions, domains and social links, and how ownership will be transferred if the administrator changes.

## Prepare one source-of-truth profile packet

Include the public trading name and legal name, canonical domain, a plain description of the product, the regions actually served, official social profiles and a logo you have permission to use. Link evidence for audit or permission claims with the correct product, entity and date. Do not copy a regulator's name into a badge that implies endorsement.

Avoid unsupported totals and generic claims such as worldwide approval. If prices are quote-based, say so instead of inventing an entry price. Keep the person responsible for updates and a change log.

## Measure the channel without confusing outcomes

Track referral visits, qualified enquiries and profile accuracy separately. Use campaign parameters where a platform permits them. AI mentions and visible citations are different observations; a profile can be accurate without appearing in a particular generated answer.
