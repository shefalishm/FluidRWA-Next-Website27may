## What to check

Map issuance, distribution, custody, trading and settlement separately. Record the entity and jurisdiction for each activity.

## Work through an example

Ask counsel to review a concrete offer and transfer scenario, including a buyer outside the issuer's jurisdiction. Do not assume one approval covers every role.
