## Start With the Operating Model, Not the Logo

Securitize, Tokeny and Taurus are all relevant to asset tokenization, but they are not the same kind of vendor. A buyer comparing them should first decide what it is actually buying.

Some issuers want a regulated operating stack for digital securities. Some want token software that fits into their own legal, custody and investor infrastructure. Some banks want custody, tokenization and marketplace infrastructure in one institutional environment.

That difference matters more than a generic "top tokenization platform" list.

## Securitize: Best Fit for Regulated Digital Securities Workflows

Securitize is most relevant when the buyer is issuing or managing regulated digital securities, tokenized funds or private-market products where investor onboarding, lifecycle management and regulated infrastructure matter.

The key reason Securitize appears in many institutional tokenization conversations is not simply that it can create tokens. It is that its model is closer to an operating ecosystem for digital securities. Buyers should review the exact scope carefully because regulated services, transfer-agent functions, issuer workflows and market access are not the same thing.

Securitize may be a strong fit when the buyer needs:

- Digital securities issuance workflows
- Investor onboarding and investor account infrastructure
- Compliance-aware transfer controls
- Tokenized fund or private securities operations
- A provider familiar with regulated product expectations
- Potential connectivity to regulated-market infrastructure

It may be less ideal when the buyer wants a purely modular token engine, wants to control every partner independently, or is building an internal enterprise asset registry without securities distribution.

The buyer questions are practical:

- Which entity provides each regulated service?
- Who owns investor records?
- How does the platform handle transfer restrictions?
- Which custody options are supported?
- Can data be exported if the issuer changes providers?
- What is the ongoing cost after launch?

## Tokeny: Best Fit for Permissioned Token Infrastructure

Tokeny is strongest for buyers that want a modular permissioned-token platform, especially where identity, compliance rules and controlled transferability matter.

Tokeny is closely associated with T-REX and ERC-3643-style infrastructure. That makes it useful for issuers that want token-level compliance controls, identity-linked permissions and a clearer separation between token software and the rest of the operating stack.

Tokeny may be a strong fit when the buyer needs:

- Permissioned tokens
- Onchain identity and eligibility controls
- Issuer-managed transfer restrictions
- A modular vendor stack
- Integration with outside KYC, custody, distribution and legal partners
- A framework that can support regulated asset workflows without requiring one bundled operating provider

It may be less ideal when the buyer wants the tokenization provider to handle most of the regulated service layer, distribution network, investor servicing and post-issuance operations directly.

The buyer should ask:

- Which identity provider will supply investor claims?
- Who updates eligibility when investor status changes?
- How are transfer rules changed after launch?
- What happens if a compliance rule conflicts with secondary-market liquidity?
- Who handles investor reporting, distributions and redemptions?

## Taurus: Best Fit for Banks and Institutional Digital Asset Programs

Taurus is most relevant when the buyer is a bank, broker, asset manager or financial institution that wants custody, tokenization and marketplace infrastructure inside a more institutional digital asset stack.

Taurus should not be evaluated only as a token issuance tool. Its broader value is in combining digital asset custody infrastructure with tokenization and marketplace capabilities. That can matter for banks because tokenization cannot be separated from custody governance, account controls, compliance, signer policies and institutional operating procedures.

Taurus may be a strong fit when the buyer needs:

- Institutional digital asset custody
- Token issuance and asset lifecycle infrastructure
- Marketplace or distribution workflows
- Bank-grade governance and controls
- A platform suitable for regulated financial institutions
- Support for tokenization as part of a broader digital asset strategy

It may be less ideal for a small issuer that only wants to launch a lightweight token quickly and does not need the full institutional custody and infrastructure layer.

The buyer should ask:

- Is custody required in the same stack as tokenization?
- Which jurisdictions and regulated entities are involved?
- Does the project need bank-grade account governance?
- Can the platform support the specific asset class and investor workflow?
- What integrations are available for compliance, reporting and distribution?

## Which One Should You Shortlist?

Shortlist Securitize if the center of gravity is regulated digital securities issuance, investor operations and lifecycle management.

Shortlist Tokeny if the center of gravity is permissioned token infrastructure, transfer restrictions and issuer-controlled compliance logic.

Shortlist Taurus if the center of gravity is institutional custody, bank-grade controls and a broader digital asset platform.

Many real projects will still need more than one vendor category. A tokenization platform may need a custodian, KYC/KYB provider, blockchain analytics tool, legal counsel, transfer agent, payment rail, smart contract auditor and reporting workflow.

## Buying Checklist

Before selecting a tokenization platform, define:

- Asset type and legal rights
- Issuer jurisdiction
- Investor jurisdictions
- Investor eligibility rules
- Primary issuance process
- Secondary transfer policy
- Custody model
- Settlement currency
- Reporting and tax workflow
- Data export rights
- Smart contract audit requirements
- Vendor exit plan

The strongest shortlist is the one that makes the operating model visible before pricing discussions begin.
