## What to check

Prepare an event ledger for issuance, transfer, distributions and redemption. Document the entity, dates, currencies and valuation source for each event.

## Work through an example

Give a tax adviser a sample transaction from subscription through disposal. Resolve classification and reporting questions before applying rules to the rest of the dataset.
