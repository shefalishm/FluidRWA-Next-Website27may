## Where does each company fit?

### Applied Blockchain: enterprise and privacy work

Applied Blockchain states it was founded in London, maintains its [headquarters there and a development hub in Porto](https://appliedblockchain.com/about), and describes [blockchain services](https://appliedblockchain.com/services). For a buyer, the important question is not the office label but who designs, builds, reviews and operates each component. Ask for a project-specific architecture showing where confidential data is held, how ledger state is verified, and who can change the deployed system.

### Soken: development with security and legal workstreams

[Soken](https://soken.dev/) publishes a London address and lists Web3 development, smart-contract audits and legal structuring. That combination may be relevant when a token project has multiple workstreams. Do not assume one contract covers all of them. Ask which legal jurisdiction is in scope, whether a security review is independent of the development team, and what report or opinion will be delivered.

### Ethereal Labs: on-chain product engineering

[Ethereal Labs](https://www.ethereallabs.io/) describes itself as a London Web3 studio building smart contracts and full-stack applications. For a launch-focused brief, request a walk-through of contract testing, upgrade controls, wallet flows, monitoring and incident procedures. Vendor-reported project numbers should not substitute for evidence from the proposed team.

## Five questions a UK buyer should settle before contracting

1. **Who is the contracting entity?** Confirm the entity signing the statement of work and where engineers and subcontractors work.
2. **Which decisions need independent advice?** Separate technical design from legal characterisation, financial promotion and compliance judgments.
3. **How are security duties split?** The developer, independent auditor and operating team should have explicit scopes and handoffs.
4. **What is the production definition of done?** Require tests, documentation, monitoring, access handover and an incident runbook, not just deployed contracts.
5. **What happens after launch?** Agree on maintenance, chain upgrades, vulnerability response and repository access.

## Buyer checklist for tokenization projects

Ask every shortlisted firm to show how investor onboarding, permissions, asset servicing, custody and redemption connect to the contracts. A token-minting demonstration alone is not a functioning asset platform.
