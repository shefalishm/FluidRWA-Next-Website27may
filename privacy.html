Information we collect

FluidRWA may collect information you submit through website forms, including name, email address, company name, website, phone number, country, LinkedIn profile, vendor details, project requirements, and messages.

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, review vendor applications, route buyer requests, manage paid memberships, provide support, process cancellation or refund requests, and improve the FluidRWA website and directory experience.

Data sharing

FluidRWA may share necessary information with service providers that help operate the website, process payments, store form submissions, send notifications, or provide analytics. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and requests

FluidRWA keeps information for as long as reasonably needed for business, support, legal, billing, and website operations. You may contact contact@fluidrwa.com to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
