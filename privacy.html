Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Trust depends on how we handle information as well as how we review vendors. This policy explains how FluidRWA uses information shared through vendor vetting applications, buyer and project enquiries, investor-interest forms and other contact with us.

Contact details

For privacy, support, billing, cancellation, or refund questions, contact FluidRWA at contact@fluidrwa.com.

Information we collect

We collect the details you choose to submit, such as your name, email, role, company, website, country, contact details, vendor category, project requirements and messages. A vetting application may also include team information, customer references, documentation, audit reports and other supporting evidence you provide.

We may retain submission timestamps, consent records, the page where an enquiry started and campaign or referral information to understand and respond to requests. Please provide only information you are entitled to share. Do not include passwords, wallet seed phrases or other access credentials.

Vetting and public company information

We use application information and relevant public sources to review a company and its claims, ask follow-up questions and communicate the outcome. Company details intended for a public vendor profile, relevant supporting sources and an approved Vetted status may appear on the website.

Internal correspondence, billing records and material supplied only for a private review are not published as part of a vendor profile. If you are submitting evidence that must remain private, tell us when you provide it.

Billing information

Payment details are processed by the relevant payment provider. FluidRWA may receive transaction references, payer contact details, payment status and related billing records needed to administer services and refunds. We do not store full card or bank account details on the website.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, carry out vetting reviews, assess relevant vendor options for a project, deliver agreed digital services, handle support and corrections, administer billing and refunds, and improve the website.

We do not share a buyer's contact details or project brief with a vendor without the buyer's permission. A request to be introduced to a named vendor will be handled within the scope of that request.

Investor updates

If you submit the investor-interest form, we collect your name, email, investor type, region, asset interests, optional allocation size and company, consent record, submission time and originating page. With your consent, we use these details to send weekly tokenized asset updates and related investor updates through our email service provider. This does not open an investment account or establish eligibility for an offering. You can withdraw consent using an update's unsubscribe option, by replying to an update, or by emailing contact@fluidrwa.com.

Storage and service providers

Website form submissions are stored privately in Cloudflare and may be sent to the FluidRWA team through internal email notifications. Necessary information may also be processed by service providers that operate the website, send email, process payments or provide analytics.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information where needed to fulfil your request, operate our services, meet applicable legal obligations or protect against misuse, subject to the permissions described in this policy.

Website analytics

Our analytics tools may collect page visits, traffic sources, device and browser information and interactions such as form starts or submissions. Form-event tracking may include limited category and workflow information, rather than contact fields or free-text messages. Browser settings can limit cookies and some forms of tracking.

Retention and requests

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to handle your request, maintain relevant review and service records, meet legal or billing obligations and operate the website. You may email contact@fluidrwa.com to request access, correction or deletion of your information, or to withdraw consent to updates. Some records may need to be retained for applicable legal or operational requirements.

Questions and corrections

If a public profile includes information that should be corrected, or you have a concern about how we have used your details, contact contact@fluidrwa.com with the relevant page address and your request.
