## Evidence handling

- FINRA firm PDFs were retrieved directly on the check date, not inferred from register homepages. The reports identify current SEC and SRO registrations and business activities. IAPD is an adviser register, not a second broker-dealer permission; no investment-adviser claim is made about these broker-dealers.
- OCC list available through web retrieval is dated September 30, 2026. It is not silently relabelled November 18. Direct curl retrieval failed twice with a connection reset; dated bank entries and provider entity mappings are the basis of the included custody lines.
- FINMA PDF downloads show November 18, 2026. The web index had older copies; the directly downloaded documents were used for the final decisions.
- The SEC summaries for Ondo and Superstate and the FCA Archax permissions were read in a live browser. Form ADV dates are filing dates, not dates of register approval.
- Public descriptions omit licence details, commercial listing explanations, rankings and financial totals. Zodia scope is explicitly Abu Dhabi. Rockefeller Capital Management is not included.
- No CSS, components, Cloudflare configuration, production deployment or merge is part of this change.
