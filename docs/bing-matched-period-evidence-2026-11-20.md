**Important:** Bing describes its page table as a sample of overall activity. The chart totals and the exported-row sums are different measures. An absent row is not proof that the page received zero citations in every AI response. These exports do not map individual queries to pages or identify replacement sources.

`data/bing-citation-period-comparison-2026-11-20.json` preserves every compared URL, the periods and export hashes. Its zero value for an absent later row is a comparison placeholder; `status: not_in_later_export` and the report caveat must accompany any interpretation.
