## Evidence rules

- **Date:** All browsing and evidence assessment in these tables was performed 2026-11-09. This is the access date, not the source publication date. Where material, source dates are given in the notes.
- **Substantiated** means a directly observed website, redirect, register entry, documented product behavior, or named counterparty statement at the scope indicated. **Vendor-reported** means a service menu, published feature, claim, case study, legal notice or self-disclosed location, not independently tested. Ordinary product/service descriptions in the third column default to vendor-reported unless a stronger source is explicitly identified.
- **Unverified** means insufficient public evidence here, not false. Unless a row explicitly states otherwise, current entity status, listed headquarters, founding date, coverage, client names and results, limits and quantified claims remain unverified. “Global” is not a headquarters, jurisdictional clearance or delivery guarantee. A registered office is not necessarily operational HQ; a brand launch date is not incorporation. A counterparty publication evidences only the named use or experiment it describes.
- **Hands-on scope for every row:** read-only public website, documentation, filing and report review. No accounts, API calls to products, wallet connections, event bookings, purchases, form submissions, vendor contact, campaign runs, transactions or benchmarking. A crawler error does not establish a site or business is inactive. No comparative performance or security outcome was verified.
- **Priorities:** P1 = material current-offer, trust or risk wording; P1 fixed = demonstrated stale public copy corrected locally; P2 = substantiate, date, attribute or narrow before relying on the claim; P3 = limited scope confirmation for ordinary procurement. Unverified claims were not treated as demonstrably false.
