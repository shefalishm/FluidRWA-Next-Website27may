# FluidRWA content and business growth plan

Prepared 7 November 2026. Planning targets below are not forecasts, promised rankings or booked revenue. No new articles are authorised or published by this memo.

## What progress can honestly be claimed

The September 6 plan preserves GSC August 7-September 3: 308 clicks, 166,983 impressions and approximately 0.184% CTR. The supplied November review reports October 7-November 3: 496 clicks, approximately 180,000 impressions, displayed 0.3% CTR and average position 13.2. This is about 61% more clicks and 8% more impressions across two different 28-day windows, not a controlled attribution study. With rounded impressions the later computed CTR is about 0.276%; the displayed 0.3% is consistent with rounding. Do not infer a May-to-November growth rate: a comparable May analytics baseline has not been established. A May website archive proves site history, not traffic.

Supplied GA4 October 9-November 5: 6,986 sessions, 1,007 engaged sessions, 14.41% engagement, 6 seconds mean engagement and 14 key events. These figures are supplied review evidence, not a newly exported dashboard in this task. The reported top-page cohorts (organic: 211 sessions, 57% engagement, 53 seconds; Direct: 187, 14%, 4 seconds) are not site-wide acquisition totals. Their exact report filters/window should be retained before comparison. The October 28 saved audit independently shows stronger organic than Direct engagement in October 21-27; it does not establish that all Direct traffic is automated.

The supplied Bing figures of roughly 82,000 citations and 63 clicks cover a different reporting period. Citations are visibility, not visitors, enquiries or purchases; do not compute a combined Google/Bing conversion rate. Fourteen GA key events do not mean fourteen qualified buyers or paying vendors. No revenue ledger was reviewed, so revenue, paid-vendor growth and commercial return remain unverified.

The defensible conclusion: search acquisition has improved since the saved late-summer baseline, useful research and buying workflows now exist, but the evidence does not yet demonstrate a repeatable commercial engine. More publishing alone is not the answer.

## November-January execution

November 7-20: establish the accepted-enquiry and qualification baseline from the private dashboards. Keep buyer, vendor, general contact, newsletter and tests separate. Record source, destination, accepted record, notification status, qualification, owner and outcome without publishing personal information. Preserve organic/Direct segmentation and investigate attribution without deleting history or blocking countries.

November 21-30: assess November 6 changes only after 14 full days (earliest full-window review November 21), with matched prior/post 14-day page-query-device cohorts. New enquiry dashboards also need at least 14 full days from their actual launch date. An early November 7 check is operational QA, not impact evidence. Report impressions, position and query mix alongside CTR; a title change is not proven causal by an aggregate increase.

December: allocate approximately 60% editorial effort to evidence-backed refreshes, 25% to distribution/permission-based partner work and 15% to one differentiated lab. This is a capacity recommendation, not measured optimal allocation. Refresh no more than two high-opportunity pages per week; publish at most one new lab every two weeks after evidence and overlap review. Keep a change log and original product-verification dates separate from layout changes.

January: expand only the journeys producing qualified conversations. Prepare vendor proposals with specific paid deliverables and actual exposure reports; never sell guaranteed leads, editorial ranking or implied endorsement. Follow up genuine enquiries within two working days. No outreach or commercial commitments are made by this document.

## January 2027 scorecard

Measure a complete 28-day window ending January 31 against the stated October/November baseline. These are ambitious operating targets, not predictions. Rebase if source definitions change.

| Measure | Base target | Stretch target | Evidence required |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Google organic clicks | 900 | 1,200 | GSC, same property and comparable filters; approximately 1.8x/2.4x the supplied 496 |
| Search CTR | 0.45% | 0.60% | Page/query/device mix and position reported alongside CTR |
| Qualified buyer enquiries in 28 days | 5 | 10 | Deduplicated accepted records with real scope, buyer identity and agreed next step; baseline still to establish |
| Vendor commercial conversations in 28 days | 10 | 20 | Substantive two-way conversations, not sent emails |
| Newly paid vendor engagements by January 31 | 2 | 4 | Signed scope and reconciled payment; no assumed price or revenue |

At unchanged 180,000 impressions, 0.45% CTR yields 810 clicks and 0.60% yields 1,080. The 900/1,200 click targets therefore also require roughly 200,000 impressions at those CTRs. These are arithmetic scenarios, not independent guarantees. Do not promise 10x traffic or set total GA sessions as the primary success measure while low-engagement Direct traffic obscures quality.

Decision gates: if two matched review cycles show no useful page-to-directory or enquiry progression, examine intent, offer and friction before adding volume. If accepted enquiries exist but do not qualify, revise audience/brief questions. If qualified conversations do not convert commercially, inspect offer, pricing and follow-up, not merely SEO. Revenue reporting starts only when the ledger is reconciled.
