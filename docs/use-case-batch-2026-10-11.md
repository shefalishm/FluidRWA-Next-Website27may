# Three researched company use cases

Research and update date: October 11, 2026.

## Selection and boundaries

- HSBC Gold Token: retail physical-asset distribution, distinct from generic provenance and tokenized-gold vendor comparisons.
- UBS uMINT: fund subscription/redemption lifecycle, distinct from the existing BUIDL treasury/collateral case.
- Broadridge DLR: immobilized collateral and integrated post-trade infrastructure, distinct from the bank-led Kinexys intraday-financing case.
- Public primary records establish launches and stated outcomes. Company evidence is not presented as independent assurance. Buyer lessons are labeled as FluidRWA interpretation.
- No vendor ranking, directory redesign, form/database changes or external promotional links. Source titles and exact URLs appear as plain-text research records.
