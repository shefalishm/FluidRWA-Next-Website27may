FluidRWA Policies

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Vetting and visibility are separate services

There is a fee for the FluidRWA vetting review. Marketing, visibility and advertising are separate services with separate fees. Paying a fee does not guarantee approval or a Vetted badge. Apply to be a Vetted Vendor.

General refund policy

Fees are non-refundable, except for the unsuccessful-vetting review described below or where a refund is required by applicable law. Cancelling a service does not by itself create an entitlement to a refund.

If you do not pass the vetting review

If your company does not pass the FluidRWA vetting review, we retain US$85 from the vetting fee paid to cover the review work. The remaining balance of that vetting fee will be refunded within 30 business days of our notifying you of the unsuccessful review outcome.

This exception applies to the vetting review fee. Marketing, visibility and advertising services are agreed separately and are not part of the vetting review.

Cancellation and future services

To cancel a service or any future renewal, email contact@fluidrwa.com. Cancellation does not reverse a completed review or automatically refund fees already paid. The unsuccessful-vetting exception above still applies.

Refund processing and support

Refunds under this policy are returned to the original payment method. For questions about a review outcome, cancellation or refund, email contact@fluidrwa.com.

This policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.
