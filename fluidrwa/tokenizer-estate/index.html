Tokenizer.Estate

Tokenizer.Estate provides white-label software for real estate owners who want to issue tokens and onboard investors under their own brand. The provider is based in Dubai, UAE and operates as Infinity Bytes Software Design L.L.C.

What the platform covers

  • Branded issuance: A client-branded offering page, investor portal and administration tools.
  • Investor onboarding: The applicant describes configurable investor groups, eligibility checks and transfer restrictions. Confirm who handles exceptions.
  • Payments and launch coordination: The provider describes payment integrations, stablecoin options and referrals to legal, banking and payment partners. Confirm currencies, settlement flows and contracting entities.

Licensing and audit scope

Tokenizer.Estate states that it does not hold investment licenses. Its jurisdiction overview describes the service as software and partner referrals, not legal or regulatory guidance. Confirm project-specific requirements with qualified counsel.

Hacken's December 2, 2025 report covers an Ethereum Solidity token contract, with final reviewed commit 8593776. Ask the provider to map that scope to your deployment. It does not cover the entire platform.

What to confirm before shortlisting

  1. Request a responsibility matrix for issuance, eligibility, payments and recordkeeping.
  2. Ask for a demo showing what happens when an investor is ineligible, a payment fails or a transfer is blocked.
  3. Confirm the fees, relevant partner agreements and how to export records if you switch providers.
