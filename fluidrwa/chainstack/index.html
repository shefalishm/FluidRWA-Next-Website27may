Quick answer

What is Chainstack?

Chainstack is a managed blockchain infrastructure provider.

It gives development and operations teams RPC access, dedicated nodes, historical data and deployment controls without requiring them to run every blockchain node internally. Its main buyer advantage is the breadth of deployment models: teams can begin with shared elastic endpoints and move to dedicated, unlimited or self-hosted infrastructure as workload, isolation and governance requirements increase.

Buyer fit

When Chainstack is a strong fit

Consider Chainstack when you need

  • One infrastructure relationship across multiple public blockchain networks.
  • A migration path from shared RPC to dedicated or self-hosted nodes.
  • Archive data, WebSockets or historical event retrieval for supported chains.
  • Managed operations and 24/7 support instead of an internal node operations team.
  • Regional deployment options and infrastructure choices for production applications.

Consider alternatives or a mixed stack when

  • Your application depends on a niche chain or RPC method that is not supported.
  • You need a fully decentralized RPC marketplace rather than a managed provider.
  • Your institution requires a specific cloud, country, certification or contractual service level not available for the selected plan.
  • You need custody, transfer agency, identity, sanctions screening or legal services in addition to blockchain connectivity.
  • A single-chain specialist delivers materially better performance or economics for your workload.

RWA and tokenization

Where Chainstack fits in an asset tokenization stack

Chainstack supplies blockchain connectivity. It can be important infrastructure, but it is not the complete tokenization operating stack.

Issuance and transfers

Applications can submit token issuance, transfer, redemption and lifecycle transactions through supported RPC endpoints.

Historical records

Archive access can support event reconstruction, balances, transaction history, reporting and operational investigations.

Monitoring and reconciliation

Teams can read contract events and chain state for internal monitoring, ledger reconciliation and exception workflows.

Multi-chain products

Broad network support can reduce infrastructure fragmentation when a product deploys across several chains.

Investor applications

RPC and WebSocket access can power dashboards, transaction status, portfolio views and controlled workflow interfaces.

What it does not replace

Buyers still need appropriate custody, identity, compliance, legal, transfer-agent, oracle and security controls.

Security and operations

Controls buyers should examine

Chainstack describes security operations, vulnerability and patch management, backup and disaster-recovery practices, and infrastructure monitoring. It also announced SOC 2 Type II certification in December 2025. These are useful diligence signals, not a guarantee that a customer's application or smart contracts are secure.

Request before contracting

  • Current SOC 2 report or bridge letter under an NDA.
  • Service levels, support severity definitions and incident communication process.
  • Data-center locations and subprocessors relevant to your deployment.
  • Access-control, API-key rotation, allowlisting and audit-log capabilities.
  • Backup, recovery-point and recovery-time commitments for dedicated nodes.

Test before production

  • Latency and error rate from every customer region.
  • Method compatibility, WebSocket stability and archive query performance.
  • Behavior during provider, region and blockchain-client outages.
  • Rate-limit and billing behavior under expected and peak traffic.
  • Failover to a second endpoint or independent provider.

Frequently asked questions

Chainstack FAQs

What is Chainstack?

Chainstack is a blockchain infrastructure provider offering managed RPC endpoints, dedicated nodes, archive data, WebSockets and self-hosted deployment options across more than 70 networks.

How many blockchain networks does Chainstack support?

Chainstack states that it supports more than 70 networks. Confirm that your required mainnet, testnet, region and node mode are available.

Does Chainstack offer dedicated and archive nodes?

Yes. Its documentation describes elastic and dedicated deployments and full, archive and trader modes, with availability depending on protocol and plan.

Does Chainstack support WebSockets?

Yes. WebSocket access appears in its published plan comparison, subject to network and deployment compatibility.

Can Chainstack be self-hosted?

Chainstack offers a self-hosted model for organizations that need managed blockchain infrastructure within their own environment.

Does Chainstack have a free plan?

At the time of review, the Developer plan was free and included 3 million request units per month, 25 RPS, one node and WebSockets.

Is Chainstack suitable for RWA and tokenization platforms?

It can support the blockchain connectivity and data layer for token issuance, transfers, historical data and monitoring. It does not replace custody, legal, identity, compliance or transfer-agent services.

Is Chainstack SOC 2 Type II certified?

Chainstack announced SOC 2 Type II certification in December 2025. Buyers should request current assurance documentation and review the scope relevant to their deployment.

What are the main Chainstack alternatives?

Alchemy, QuickNode, Infura, Ankr and Blockdaemon are common alternatives. Fit depends on chain coverage, deployment isolation, regions, support, archive requirements and price.
