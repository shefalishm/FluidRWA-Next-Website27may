Blocknative API Sunset and Migration

Blocknative announced that its team joined Deloitte and that its APIs and Gas Network would cease after June 19, 2026. FluidRWA retains this profile for reference and does not list those services as active options for new deployments.

What teams used it for

Blocknative historically provided transaction and mempool visibility and gas estimation. Those are separate dependencies: replacing a gas estimate endpoint does not automatically replace a pending transaction feed.

What to check before switching

  1. Inventory the endpoints, chains and alerts your application used.
  2. Test proposed replacements with representative transactions; compare coverage, lag, missed events and recovery when a connection drops.
  3. Remove retired credentials and confirm that an old integration cannot silently serve stale data.
