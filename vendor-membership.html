Questions

Vendor review FAQs

Is it free to apply?

Yes. Submitting an application does not require payment. We review complete applications before deciding whether the company is eligible for a FluidRWA visibility opportunity.

Does approval guarantee a listing?

No. Review determines eligibility. Approval does not guarantee a particular placement, ranking or scope of exposure.

No. FluidRWA provides vendor discovery, credibility and exposure opportunities. We do not guarantee leads, introductions, sales, traffic, rankings or any commercial outcome.

When will I see pricing?

Approved applicants receive a private proposal based on category fit and the appropriate mix of profile, directory, research, editorial and partnership exposure.

Can a company buy the vetted badge?

No. A company must pass FluidRWA's review. Commercial participation can determine the scope of approved exposure, but cannot purchase eligibility or endorsement.
