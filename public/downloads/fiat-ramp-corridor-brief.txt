Ask each candidate:
1. Exact product, version, operator and contracting entity?
2. Written eligibility for this customer, direction, country and rail combination?
3. Partner KYB, end-user KYC, beneficiary validation and screening owners?
4. Production access, rail endorsements and prerequisites?
5. Supported tokens, networks, wallet model and confirmation rules?
6. Quote type, expiry, funding deadline, deductions and net delivery?
7. Funds-in-transit, account holder, ledger and withdrawal rights?
8. Meaning of each status from funding to recipient credit?
9. Treatment of late funding, incorrect amount and wrong-network transfers?
10. Rejected-payout return currency, network, address, timing and support owner?
11. Webhook authenticity, duplicates, replay and status retrieval?
12. Order, chain, bank-reference and refund reconciliation exports?
13. Liquidity, prefunding, limits and outage procedures?
14. Exit process, open-order reconciliation and record access?
