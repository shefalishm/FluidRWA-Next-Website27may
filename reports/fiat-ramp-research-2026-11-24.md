## Primary evidence

See `fiat-ramp-primary-evidence-2026-11-24.json` for exact links, reporting dates and limits. Important findings: Transak Whitelabel API limitations are not company-wide off-ramp absence; Banxa Native API is Beta and agreement-based; Ramp sell requires enabled production access; Stripe Onramp is distinct from Bridge; MoonPay PIX buy support does not establish PIX payout; Circle Mint's current institutional wording excludes personal individual use; Bridge's rate endpoint is an estimate, not a locked quote. Bridge BRL guide retains the June 15, 2026 endorsement-change date. Bitso Business source is August 20, 2025, not 2026.

No funds moved, no authenticated payment APIs tested, no blanket regulatory conclusions published. Historical Network Explorer ramp evidence is byte-for-byte unchanged. A source being available does not prove account approval, transaction success or authorization for a buyer's route.
