## Editorial strategy and limitations

Retain the established URL and useful prior content. Add a clear opening, ten alphabetized major-practice examples, seven asset-specific mandates, six responsibility allocations, a twelve-question RFP, six legal-to-technical acceptance scenarios, local-law/entity checks and post-launch change controls. Remove outbound vendor hyperlinks while preserving exact evidence URLs in this internal report. Neutral institutional links remain. No new Vetted status, price figures or client-listing changes.

The earlier guide already covered classification, custody, tax, disclosures and transfers. Its principal gap was actionable comparison: few named examples, no concise scope matrix, little engagement diligence and no testable implementation handover. These are relevance hypotheses, not a demonstrated ranking cause. More words do not guarantee citations. Reassess the same query and GSC page metrics after recrawl; compare matched periods and record actual citation destinations separately from traffic.
